## Master architecture

~~~text
PSYCHOLOGICAL CLOSURE
      ↓
narrows accessible psychological territory
      +
narrows admissible self-model
      +
narrows boundary of the search space
      ↓
OPENING
      ↓
previously excluded possibility becomes
noticeable / nameable / interpretable / admissible
      ↓
EXPANSION OF THE CONSCIOUS MIND
      ↓
possible durable expansion of the self-model
~~~

The theory is about **access and hypothesis space**, not a claim that consciousness literally creates all newly encountered material.

## Three layers that must remain separate

### 1. Territory

[Psychological territory](../entries/psychological-territory.md) asks:

> What classes of inner experience can become objects of reflection?

### 2. Search space

[Boundary of the search space](../entries/boundary-of-the-search-space.md) asks:

> Which possibilities are seriously generated as hypotheses at all?

### 3. Admissibility

[Admissible self-model](../entries/admissible-self-model.md) asks:

> Which visible possibilities are permitted to count as genuine evidence about the self?

These layers can diverge.

~~~text
visible
    ≠
considered as a live hypothesis
    ≠
admitted into the self-model
    ≠
approved for action
~~~

This separation is one of the main protections against both denial and overinterpretation.

## Closure

[Psychological closure](../entries/psychological-closure.md) is the inverse pressure.

It can operate through:

- moral exclusion;
- identity protection;
- shame;
- social-role expectations;
- unavailable vocabulary;
- rapid reinterpretation;
- simple failure to generate a possibility.

Closure is deliberately broader than any one clinical mechanism.

The key warning is:

> **Denial is not automatically evidence of closure.**

The concept must remain falsifiable and cannot authorize outsiders to claim privileged access to another person's “real” self.

## Opening

[Opening](../entries/opening.md) is the discontinuous transition mechanism.

~~~text
formerly unavailable possibility
        ↓
becomes accessible
        ↓
enters reflective search
        ↓
may update admissible self-model
~~~

The strongest diagnostic question is:

> **Did the experience enlarge the future search space of the self?**

This makes opening more specific than emotional intensity.

## Opening versus disinhibition

[Disinhibition](../entries/disinhibition.md) lowers restraint.

Opening changes accessibility.

~~~text
DISINHIBITION
temporary reduction of constraint

OPENING
new psychological possibility becomes available

DURABLE EXPANSION
possibility remains available after initiating state ends
~~~

One episode can move through all three stages, but none should be inferred automatically from the previous one.

## Opening versus maintenance

[Maintenance](../entries/maintenance.md) preserves an existing range of functioning or access.

Opening expands the map.

~~~text
maintenance:
keep the current system usable

opening:
alter what the current system can represent
~~~

A mechanism can begin as opening and later become maintenance.

That role change is not evidence that the mechanism stopped working.

## Diminishing returns

[Diminishing returns of opening](../entries/diminishing-returns-of-opening.md) captures a local hypothesis:

> **A continent can only be discovered once.**

Once a major possibility boundary has moved, repeating the same event cannot make that exact possibility newly conceivable again.

The informational problem shifts from discovery toward:

- differentiation;
- integration;
- retention;
- exploration within known territory.

The principle remains provisional because new contexts can expose genuinely new boundaries.

## Durable expansion

[Durable expansion of the self-model](../entries/durable-expansion-of-the-self-model.md) supplies the persistence criterion.

~~~text
opening event ends
      ↓
new possibility remains thinkable
      ↓
future self-analysis can still use it
~~~

This does not require permanent identity commitment or endorsement.

The minimum residue is:

> **I can no longer honestly treat this possibility as psychologically impossible for me.**

## Colonization metaphor

[Colonization of the subconscious](../entries/colonization-of-the-subconscious.md) is preserved as a strong user-authored metaphor for the larger expansion process.

Its mapping is:

~~~text
inner material       → territory
introspection        → reconnaissance
categories           → mapping tools
self-interpretation  → administration
integration          → incorporation
~~~

But the metaphor is explicitly bounded by **self-opacity**.

Increased administrative reach is not complete conscious sovereignty.

## Discovery versus creation remains unresolved

The opening model deliberately does not decide whether newly accessible material is:

- discovered;
- constructed;
- socially produced;
- state-dependent;
- latent;
- some mixture.

That question remains queued as **discovery versus creation of the self** and belongs to a later batch.

The current cluster protects only the narrower claim:

> **The space of consciously available self-interpretation changed.**

## Bridge to the next self-legibility branch

Opening creates the possibility of later recursive analysis.

The remaining queued material asks what happens after inner territory becomes available:

~~~text
opening
  ↓
legibility
  ↓
self-legibility
  ↓
recursive introspection
  ↓
investigating the investigator
  ↓
internal tribunal
  ↓
procedural self-analysis
~~~

Those concepts remain intentionally unpromoted in this batch so the mechanics of opening are not collapsed into the governance of introspection.

## Research frontier

- Which opening mechanisms produce the most durable self-model change?
- Can a useful measure distinguish search-space expansion from mere novelty?
- When does maintenance preserve an expansion versus restore closure?
- Which forms of psychological closure are adaptive?
- How should state-dependent evidence be weighted?
- Can repeated opening create new territory after the original boundary saturates?
- How much self-model expansion depends on socially available language?
