# Institutions, Dependency, and Legibility

This cluster tracks a recurring question:

> **When modern institutions free people from personal or local dependency, what new dependencies and representations replace it?**

## Working sequence

```text
dense local / kin relation
        ↓
social disembedding
        ↓
portable formal relation
        ↓
institutional / market mediation
        ↓
institutional legibility
        ↓
shift dependency
```

This sequence is not assumed to occur in every case. It is a research hypothesis and comparison tool.

## Central normative variable: contestability

The important comparison is rarely “dependency versus independence.”

It is more often:

- dependency on whom;
- through what mechanism;
- how concentrated;
- how visible;
- how challengeable;
- with what exit options;
- with what rights;
- at what cost;
- and what local capacities disappear during the transfer.
