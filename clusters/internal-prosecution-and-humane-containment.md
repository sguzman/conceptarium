## Governing distinction

~~~text
EVIDENCE
"What is present?"

INTERPRETATION
"What does it mean?"

CONTAINMENT
"What behavior or authority must be limited?"

PUNISHMENT
"What suffering is justified?"

CRUELTY
"What suffering is added beyond what truth
or containment requires?"
~~~

The central rule is:

> **An internal fact can be evidence without becoming a sentence.**

## Prosecutorial drift

[Internal prosecution](../entries/internal-prosecution.md) occurs when inquiry is organized around proving guilt.

The failure can begin subtly:

~~~text
motive appears
    ↓
motive is treated as suspicious
    ↓
ambiguity is resolved against the part
    ↓
explanation becomes prosecution
~~~

The problem is procedural, not merely emotional.

## Internal Inquisition

[Internal Inquisition](../entries/internal-inquisition.md) is the totalized form.

~~~text
everything may be examined
        ↓ corrupted into
everything is suspect
        ↓ corrupted into
everything must constantly justify its existence
~~~

Universal analytical jurisdiction therefore requires strong stopping and anti-cruelty constraints.

## Analytical cruelty

[Analytical cruelty](../entries/analytical-cruelty.md) is broader than [Cruelty in the tribunal](../entries/cruelty-in-the-tribunal.md).

Cruelty can enter during:

- questioning;
- diagnosis;
- interpretation;
- provenance tracing;
- exposure;
- adjudication.

The broader concept matters because punitive surplus can appear before any formal verdict.

## Evidence versus punishment

[Evidence versus punishment](../entries/evidence-versus-punishment.md) separates three questions:

1. What is present?
2. What does it mean?
3. What response is justified?

The first two do not automatically answer the third.

This implements the constitutional axiom:

[Nothing in me is automatically condemned by examination.](../entries/nothing-in-me-is-automatically-condemned-by-examination.md)

## Containment without cruelty

Some internal phenomena genuinely require limits.

[Containment without cruelty](../entries/containment-without-cruelty.md) permits:

- refusal;
- delay;
- environmental controls;
- loss of executive authority;
- behavioral boundaries;
- monitoring.

But it rejects the conversion:

~~~text
needs containment
        ↓ invalid inference
deserves humiliation
~~~

The contained part can retain standing without retaining executive control.

## Relation to Containment

The registry already preserves **Containment** in the Internal-agent phenomenology / self-governance queue:

> Firmly restricting a self-state's behavior or executive power when necessary without denying its existence or humiliating it.

The confessional queue also contained **Firm containment**, which is functionally the same object.

Therefore **Firm containment should not be independently materialized**.

It belongs on consolidation hold until Conceptarium gains a lossless alias/merge state.
