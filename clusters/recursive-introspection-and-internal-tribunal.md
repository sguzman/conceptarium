## The investigator becomes part of the case

The crucial recursive move is:

~~~text
everything in me is investigable
        ↓
therefore
the investigator is investigable
        ↓
therefore
the tribunal is investigable
        ↓
therefore
the procedure is investigable
~~~

This breaks the hidden asymmetry in which the reflective authority treats itself as the one transparent and neutral part of the psyche.

[Recursive introspection](../entries/recursive-introspection.md) is the general mechanism.

[Investigating the investigator](../entries/investigating-the-investigator.md) is the focused operation.

## Constitutional anti-immunity rule

The governing phrase is:

> **No part of me gets to say: do not investigate me.**

This creates an internal rule of contestability.

No identity, motive, evaluator, belief, interpretation, or procedure receives permanent exemption merely because it currently occupies a position of authority.

The rule directly opposes the still-queued concept **epistemic immunity**.

But universal examinability is not the same thing as universal suspicion.

~~~text
contestable
    ≠
presumed guilty
~~~

## Tribunal architecture

The [Internal tribunal](../entries/internal-tribunal.md) is the adjudicative layer that gives inner claims standing.

It may decide:

- whether a state counts as part of the self;
- how much evidential weight it deserves;
- whether acting on an impulse is acceptable;
- whether a self-description is coherent;
- whether a claim remains open, rejected, constrained, or admitted.

The tribunal is broader than conscience because it adjudicates truth, authenticity, identity, evidence, and legitimacy as well as morality.

## Procedure over verdict

[Procedural self-analysis](../entries/procedural-self-analysis.md) shifts attention from:

> **What verdict did I reach?**

to:

> **Was this a fair way to reach it?**

Relevant dimensions include:

- evidentiary symmetry;
- burden of proof;
- reversibility;
- role reversal;
- provenance handling;
- proportionality;
- humane constraint.

This is the introspective analogue of procedural legitimacy.

## Anti-cruelty boundary

The cluster deliberately carries two constitutional pressures at once:

~~~text
ANTI-IMMUNITY
nothing gets permanent exemption

ANTI-CRUELTY
investigation does not create torture authority
~~~

[Cruelty in the tribunal](../entries/cruelty-in-the-tribunal.md) names the failure in which truth-seeking gains punitive surplus:

- humiliation;
- contempt;
- erasure;
- endless prosecution;
- delegitimization beyond what evidence requires.

The neighboring queued concepts **analytical cruelty**, **containment without cruelty**, and **no torture authority** will later deepen this branch.

For now, the central distinction is:

~~~text
recognition
    ≠
approval

containment
    ≠
humiliation

investigation
    ≠
punishment
~~~

## Recursive expansion

[Recursive expansion](../entries/recursive-expansion.md) names the structural consequence of making the observing machinery itself legible.

~~~text
impulse
→ interpretation
→ interpreter
→ tribunal
→ procedure
→ standards behind procedure
~~~

Each newly objectified level can expose another possible level of analysis.

This does not require infinite recursion.

It means the introspective field can grow **procedurally**, not only by discovering new psychological content.

## Legibility

[Legibility](../entries/legibility.md) is the broader cross-domain operation:

> obscure material becomes describable, categorizable, comparable, recordable, and available to reasoning.

In this cluster, recursion increases legibility by turning hidden background processes into explicit objects.

The next queued branch will specialize this inward as **legibility of the self**, **reflective administration**, **conscious sovereignty**, **retreating frontier**, and **self-opacity**.

## Core anti-collapse rules

- introspection ≠ neutrality;
- investigator ≠ whole self;
- contestability ≠ suspicion;
- procedure ≠ truth;
- harsh conclusion ≠ cruelty;
- containment ≠ erasure;
- recursion ≠ infinite regress;
- legibility ≠ complete self-knowledge.
