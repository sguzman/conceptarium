# Abundance, Pleasure, and Prestige

This cluster is the canonical map for the post-scarcity pleasure/prestige research program.

## Umbrella framework

- [Post-scarcity prestige ecology](../entries/post-scarcity-prestige-ecology.md)
- [Pleasure ecology](../entries/pleasure-ecology.md)

The framework begins from a deliberate inversion of the ordinary scarcity question.

Instead of asking:

> Who can obtain the pleasurable good?

it asks:

> What happens when nearly everyone can obtain it, repeatedly, cheaply, and with weak immediate consequences?

That change moves analytical pressure from **access** toward **response to access**.

## Backbone

The user identified the following as the strongest conceptual chain:

```text
Pleasure ecology
      ↓
Abundance discipline
      ↓
Sovereignty over abundance
      ↓
Conspicuous abstention
      ↓
Prestige migration
      ↓
Friction premium
      ↓
Synthetic friction
      ↓
Abundance sorting
```

This should be treated as a **research backbone**, not as a claim that every society must traverse these stages in a strict temporal sequence.

A more causal rendering is:

```text
abundant / cheap pleasure
        ↓
scarcity discipline weakens
        ↓
temptation environment becomes more important
        ↓
internalized governor + abundance discipline
        ↓
possible sovereignty over abundance
        ↓
pleasure saturation weakens possession as status signal
        ↓
prestige migration
        ├── conspicuous abstention
        ├── mastery over pleasure
        └── friction premium
                 ↓
        synthetic friction / luxury friction
                 ↓
heterogeneous responses accumulate
        ↓
abundance sorting
```

## Constraint branch

- [Scarcity discipline](../entries/scarcity-discipline.md)
- [Abundance discipline](../entries/abundance-discipline.md)
- [Internalized governor](../entries/internalized-governor.md)
- [Constraint substitution](../entries/constraint-substitution.md)
- [Synthetic friction](../entries/synthetic-friction.md)
- [Sovereignty over abundance](../entries/sovereignty-over-abundance.md)

Key distinction:

```text
scarcity discipline:
"I stop because the environment makes continuation difficult."

abundance discipline:
"I could continue, but a self-authored limit remains operative."
```

**Constraint substitution** is the bridge between the two worlds: instead of recreating deprivation wholesale, selected regulatory functions of scarcity can be rebuilt through chosen or contestable limits.

## Prestige branch

- [Prestige migration](../entries/prestige-migration.md)
- [Conspicuous abstention](../entries/conspicuous-abstention.md)
- [Mastery over pleasure](../entries/mastery-over-pleasure.md)
- [Abundance inversion](../entries/abundance-inversion.md)
- [Friction premium](../entries/friction-premium.md)
- [Luxury friction](../entries/luxury-friction.md)
- [Pleasure saturation](../entries/pleasure-saturation.md)

Core hypothesis:

```text
broad access
   ↓
possession becomes less discriminating
   ↓
status moves toward scarce capacities
   ↓
restraint / selectivity / difficulty / authenticity / endurance
```

The crucial distinction in **abundance inversion** is voluntary versus imposed deprivation. Eating little because food is unavailable is not the same social signal as eating little while unlimited food is visibly available.

## Sorting and embodiment branch

- [Abundance sorting](../entries/abundance-sorting.md)
- [Longevity sorting](../entries/longevity-sorting.md)
- [Behavioral legibility of health](../entries/behavioral-legibility-of-health.md)
- [Embodied history](../entries/embodied-history.md)

These concepts are especially sensitive to moral overreach.

The framework must preserve:

> **Greater behavioral legibility does not mean perfect causal legibility, and causal contribution does not imply moral deservedness.**

Current health outcomes remain massively shaped by material conditions, access, genetics, disease, stress, development, environment, and chance.

The post-scarcity claim is conditional: if many of those constraints were genuinely equalized, some behavioral contributions might become *relatively more visible*.

## Political branch

- [Abundance politics](../entries/abundance-politics.md)
- [Temptation environment](../entries/temptation-environment.md)

As allocation pressure declines, politics can move toward questions such as:

- Who designs temptation?
- How intense may engineered rewards become?
- Which frictions may institutions impose?
- What right does a person have to disable or escape optimized persuasion?
- When does public-health design become domination?
- What forms of constraint remain contestable?

This is where the cluster connects naturally to [Anti-domination](../entries/anti-domination.md) and [Contestability](../entries/contestability.md), even though those relations should be added only where the sentence-level claim is precise.

## Research cautions

1. **Post-scarcity is domain-specific.** Cheap calories do not imply abundant land, time, beauty, power, attention, or relationships.
2. **Equal access is not equal environment.** Temptation intensity and developmental history can remain unequal.
3. **Visible bodies are causally underdetermined.** Never turn embodied history into physiognomic moral judgment.
4. **Voluntary austerity is not poverty.** Luxury friction and conspicuous abstention depend on credible choice.
5. **Friction is not automatically good.** Many frictions are pure waste or domination.
6. **Canonical vocabulary is not empirical proof.** Several entries define hypotheses that now require comparison with external literature and real-world cases.

## Research frontier

High-value questions:

- Which prestige objects survive material abundance because they are intrinsically positional?
- Does attention become the master scarcity of high-abundance environments?
- Can abundance discipline be trained without recreating coercive scarcity?
- Which forms of synthetic friction support sovereignty rather than dependence?
- Does prestige migration inevitably convert restraint into a new class marker?
- Can abundance politics remain anti-dominating when it governs temptation?
- Under what conditions does health become more behaviorally legible without becoming morally overinterpreted?
