## Governing synthesis

The architecture is:

~~~text
CONFESSION
    ↓
material becomes available
    ↓
CONFESSIONAL SELF-ANALYSIS
    ↓
ANALYTICAL JURISDICTION
    ↓
nothing receives permanent epistemic immunity
    ↓
SUBPOENA POWER OF INTROSPECTION
    ↓
internal claims may be questioned
    ↓
BUT:
NO TORTURE AUTHORITY
    +
NO AUTOMATIC CONDEMNATION
    ↓
CONFESSION WITHOUT SUBMISSION
    ↓
CONFESSIONAL EPISTEMOLOGY
~~~

The constitutional formula is:

> **Universal analytical jurisdiction without torture authority.**

## Anti-immunity

Batch 13 established the exact principle:

[No part of me gets to say: do not investigate me](../entries/no-part-of-me-gets-to-say-do-not-investigate-me.md).

The present cluster gives that principle explicit constitutional machinery.

### Analytical jurisdiction

[Analytical jurisdiction](../entries/analytical-jurisdiction.md) defines the domain of possible scrutiny.

The self does not occupy an unexplained exterior.

### Epistemic immunity

[Epistemic immunity](../entries/epistemic-immunity.md) names the protected status the constitution rejects.

~~~text
stable belief
    ≠
immune belief
~~~

A claim may be durable while remaining in principle contestable.

### Self-exemption

[Self-exemption](../entries/self-exemption.md) is the outward-facing failure mode.

A person can analyze institutions, ideology, incentives, and other people while treating their own standpoint as transparent.

Recursive introspection counteracts this asymmetry.

## Subpoena power

[Subpoena power of introspection](../entries/subpoena-power-of-introspection.md) operationalizes jurisdiction.

Anything inside the domain may in principle be called forward and asked:

- what do you claim?
- what evidence supports you?
- where did you come from?
- what function do you serve?
- what jurisdiction are you claiming?
- what would change your conclusion?

The metaphor preserves a key distinction:

~~~text
may be called to testify
        ≠
is presumed guilty
~~~

## Anti-cruelty

Universal jurisdiction becomes dangerous without a second constitutional limit.

[No torture authority](../entries/no-torture-authority.md) states:

~~~text
right to investigate
        ≠
right to humiliate

right to adjudicate
        ≠
right to brutalize

need for containment
        ≠
license for erasure
~~~

This directly constrains [Cruelty in the tribunal](../entries/cruelty-in-the-tribunal.md).

## No automatic condemnation

[Nothing in me is automatically condemned by examination.](../entries/nothing-in-me-is-automatically-condemned-by-examination.md) protects the distinction between evidence production and verdict.

Together the two constitutional axioms are:

~~~text
NO IMMUNITY
nothing is automatically exempt from examination

NO AUTOMATIC CONDEMNATION
nothing is automatically guilty because it was examined
~~~

The second does not guarantee exoneration.

A phenomenon may still be false, harmful, distorted, or in need of containment after evidence is considered.

## Confession

[Confession](../entries/confession.md) makes interior material available for analysis.

In this framework confession is analytical rather than primarily penitential.

Disclosure does not by itself determine interpretation or verdict.

## Confession without submission

[Confession without submission](../entries/confession-without-submission.md) prevents disclosure from becoming a transfer of sovereignty.

~~~text
disclosure
    +
external challenge
    +
external evidence
        ≠
automatic external authorship
~~~

This preserves the earlier self-authorship architecture:

- outside interpretation may be useful;
- expertise can increase evidentiary weight;
- external correction remains possible;
- final incorporation into the self-model is not granted by status alone.

## Confessional self-analysis

[Confessional self-analysis](../entries/confessional-self-analysis.md) is the working practice.

It combines:

- disclosure;
- interpretation;
- recursive scrutiny;
- procedural review;
- provenance tracing;
- model revision.

## Confessional epistemology

[Confessional epistemology](../entries/confessional-epistemology.md) is the umbrella framework.

Its pipeline is:

~~~text
disclosure
→ narration
→ interrogation
→ provenance
→ adversarial testing
→ recursive scrutiny
→ procedural review
→ reinterpretation
~~~

It inherits the Batch 13 internal-tribunal architecture and adds an explicit disclosure/evidence-production layer.

## Relation to historical inward instrumentation

[Historical Inward Instrumentation](./historical-inward-instrumentation.md) explains one genealogy of the machinery.

Confession, interior examination, motive scrutiny, and recursive examination can be studied as historical techniques whose operations later appear in secular self-analysis.

The present cluster is normative/procedural rather than a claim of historical descent.

## Relation to self-legibility

Confessional epistemology can increase [Legibility of the self](../entries/legibility-of-the-self.md) by making otherwise excluded material:

- nameable;
- recordable;
- comparable;
- contestable;
- revisable.

It remains constrained by [Self-opacity](../entries/self-opacity.md).

## Consolidation holds

Several queued terms preserve useful wording but currently exert heavy duplicate pressure against materialized Batch 13 concepts.

They should remain registered without independent entries until Conceptarium can represent lossless alias/merge states:

- **The investigator inside the investigation** — overlaps recursive introspection and investigating the investigator.
- **No diplomatic immunity for the self** — overlaps the exact anti-immunity principle.
- **Nothing in me is exempt from examination.** — functionally restates the exact anti-immunity principle.
- **Recursive analysis** — overlaps recursive introspection.

These are preservation records, not forgotten work.

**Analytical cruelty is not on hold.** Existing canon explicitly treats it as the broader neighboring concept of which cruelty in the tribunal is an adjudicative subtype.

## Core anti-collapse rules

- confession ≠ submission;
- disclosure ≠ truth;
- disclosure ≠ guilt;
- jurisdiction ≠ suspicion;
- examinability ≠ condemnation;
- contestability ≠ permanent doubt;
- external evidence ≠ external sovereignty;
- humane procedure ≠ comforting verdicts;
- containment ≠ cruelty;
- anti-cruelty ≠ anti-judgment;
- self-authorship ≠ autarky.
