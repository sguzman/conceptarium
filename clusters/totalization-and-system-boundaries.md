## Master distinction

~~~text
TOTALIZATION
expand one framework across more reality
        ↓
        ├── epistemic boundary
        │      reality must retain veto
        │             ↓
        │   failure: PARANOID TOTALIZATION
        │
        └── social boundary
               other persons retain standing
                      ↓
          failure: TREATING PEOPLE AS COMPONENTS
~~~

The two failures are related but should not be collapsed.

An explanatory model can become self-sealing without dominating anyone.

A local institution can dominate people without possessing a grand explanatory theory.

## Productive totalization

Totalization can be intellectually valuable when it creates explanatory compression, reusable abstractions, cross-domain integration, and unified system design.

The governing question is whether the expanding framework preserves **external constraints**.

~~~text
powerful integration
      +
reality can still falsify the model
      +
persons can still contest the system
      ↓
productive large-scale architecture
~~~

## Epistemic stopping rule

[Paranoid totalization](../entries/paranoid-totalization.md) occurs when anomaly no longer constrains the model.

The corrective branch remains:

~~~text
totalizing explanation
      ↓
contradiction
      ↓
Adversarial verification
      ↓
Epistemic maturation
~~~

The key question is:

> **What observation would make the theory less likely?**

## Social stopping rule

[Treating people as components](../entries/treating-people-as-components.md) occurs when modeled persons cease to have independent standing inside the optimizing architecture.

[Sovereign system builders](../entries/sovereign-system-builders.md) supplies the reciprocal systems-language correction:

> Other people are not merely elements of my system; they are builders and maintainers of systems of their own.

The institutional form is [Anti-domination](../entries/anti-domination.md) plus contestability, exit, appeal, and boundary rules.

## Representation versus jurisdiction

[Psychic imperialism](../entries/psychic-imperialism.md) and [Imperium without jurisdiction](../entries/imperium-without-jurisdiction.md) preserve an intentionally extreme private scope:

~~~text
anything may be represented
anything may be theorized
anything may be conceptually annexed
        ↓
NO automatic authority over represented persons
~~~

This is important because the solution is not necessarily to make thought smaller.

The preferred architecture is:

> **large representational scope + weak model sovereignty before reality + strong jurisdictional limits over persons**

## Shared diagnostic

Across both epistemic and social cases, totalization becomes dangerous when it eliminates independent veto points.

~~~text
EPISTEMIC VETO
reality / evidence / contradiction

SOCIAL VETO
consent / contestability / exit / appeal / counter-power
~~~

A healthy large system therefore preserves multiple things that can still say **no** to it.

## Research frontier

- How should federated systems differ from totalizing systems?
- What architecture lets software model people richly without converting model outputs into unilateral authority?
- When does explanatory compression become category erasure?
- Can institutional contestability play the same structural role socially that falsification plays epistemically?
