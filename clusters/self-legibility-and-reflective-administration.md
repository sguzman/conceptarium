The governing synthesis is:

## Legibility versus administration versus sovereignty

These are three different layers.

### Legibility of the self

> What can be described, distinguished, compared, recorded, and reasoned about?

### Reflective administration

> What can consciousness organize, adjudicate, integrate, or govern using that legibility?

### Conscious sovereignty

> Does consciousness therefore fully rule the mind?

The first two can increase substantially while the third remains false.

~~~text
legibility
    ≠
administration

administration
    ≠
sovereignty
~~~

## Cartographic model

[Psychological cartography](../entries/psychological-cartography.md) organizes increasingly legible inner material into:

- regions;
- boundaries;
- transitions;
- conflicts;
- recurring agents;
- motives;
- unknowns.

The map should preserve uncertainty.

A psychologically useful map can contain blank regions and disputed borders.

## Self-opacity

[Self-opacity](../entries/self-opacity.md) is the hard constraint on the project.

It preserves the fact that motives, causes, memory formation, state transitions, and biological processes can remain partly inaccessible even after sophisticated introspection.

The goal is therefore not:

~~~text
opaque self
→ total transparent self
~~~

but:

~~~text
less legible self
→ more legible self
+ better uncertainty representation
~~~

## Retreating frontier

[Retreating frontier](../entries/retreating-frontier.md) supplies the dynamic form of self-opacity.

~~~text
new understanding
      ↓
new distinctions
      ↓
new questions
      ↓
newly visible unknowns
~~~

Better maps can increase awareness of ignorance.

That is progress without completion.

## Reflective administration

[Reflective administration](../entries/reflective-administration.md) names the actual positive capacity that survives rejection of sovereignty.

Consciousness may be able to:

- classify;
- compare;
- record;
- adjudicate;
- integrate;
- set behavioral boundaries;
- revise models.

This is substantial agency.

It does not require complete causal mastery.

## Psychic sovereignty is a different axis

[Psychic sovereignty](../entries/psychic-sovereignty.md) is normative:

> External authorities do not acquire title over one's private imaginative life.

[Conscious sovereignty](../entries/conscious-sovereignty.md) is descriptive/causal:

> Reflective consciousness completely governs the mind.

The framework can endorse the first while rejecting the second.
