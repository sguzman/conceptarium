# Infinite License, Unbounded Claims, and Revanchist Warrant

This cluster studies a common transformation:

~~~text
FINITE WARRANT
      ↓
INFINITE ENTITLEMENT
~~~

The warrant may be **positive**:

- achievement;
- expertise;
- credential;
- ascent;
- prestige;
- victory;
- sacrifice.

Or **negative**:

- injury;
- humiliation;
- dispossession;
- defeat;
- historical grievance.

The content differs radically, but the structural question is the same:

> **What gives this warrant its scope, duration, targets, remedy, and termination condition?**

The cluster exists to make that question explicit.

## Master mechanism: Warrant unbounding

[Warrant unbounding](../entries/warrant-unbounding.md) is the common mechanism.

~~~text
finite justification
      ↓
bounded local claim
      ↓
scope / time / target / remedy expands
      ↓
renewed justification disappears
      ↓
unbounded entitlement
~~~

A warrant can become unbounded along several axes:

- **domain** — authority spreads into unrelated areas;
- **time** — past warrant persists indefinitely;
- **revocation** — negative evidence cannot remove authority;
- **target** — liability spreads beyond responsible parties;
- **remedy** — repair expands into punishment or jurisdiction;
- **inheritance** — special claims transmit across generations.

The cluster therefore refuses a crude equation between **large** and **unbounded**. A very large claim can be bounded. A small claim can be unbounded.

## Achievement branch: Infinite license

[Infinite license](../entries/infinite-license.md) names the achievement/status branch.

The user’s motivating formulations were:

> “We have earned the right to manage you forever.”

and:

> “I have risen; therefore I get to rule you.”

The intended structure is:

~~~text
achievement / credential / ascent
        ↓
legitimate local warrant
        ↓
credential-to-jurisdiction conversion
        +
domain leakage
        +
temporal laundering
        +
revocation asymmetry
        ↓
INFINITE LICENSE
~~~

The concept is not anti-expertise.

It asks whether expertise or achievement has become **title over persons**.

This branch connects directly to earlier Conceptarium constraints:

- [Models don’t confer title](../entries/models-dont-confer-title.md)
- [Prestige without sovereignty](../entries/prestige-without-sovereignty.md)
- [Imperium without jurisdiction](../entries/imperium-without-jurisdiction.md)
- [Fallen constitutionalism](../entries/fallen-constitutionalism.md)

Those entries describe the constitutional alternative:

~~~text
capacity / prestige / understanding
        ↓
may remain real and unequal
        ✕
no automatic generalized jurisdiction
~~~

### Credential-to-jurisdiction conversion

[Credential-to-jurisdiction conversion](../entries/credential-to-jurisdiction-conversion.md) names the social conversion from qualification to governing title.

~~~text
credential proves competence in X
        ↓
deference in X
        ↓
credential prestige substitutes for new warrant
        ↓
authority over Y / persons
~~~

### Domain leakage

[Domain leakage](../entries/domain-leakage.md) is the scope mechanism.

A warrant valid in one field can drift into unrelated fields simply because prestige travels more easily than justification.

### Temporal laundering of legitimacy

[Temporal laundering of legitimacy](../entries/temporal-laundering-of-legitimacy.md) is the time mechanism.

~~~text
authority was justified then
        ↓
historical success becomes prestige
        ↓
conditions change
        ↓
authority survives as if old warrant were current
~~~

### Revocation asymmetry

[Revocation asymmetry](../entries/revocation-asymmetry.md) is the corrigibility failure:

~~~text
success → authority increases
failure → authority does not decrease
~~~

This is one route by which a finite credential becomes effectively permanent jurisdiction.

## Generic claim branch

[Unbounded claim](../entries/unbounded-claim.md) is broader than revanchism.

Its diagnostic test is:

> **What event would make the claimant acknowledge that the special claim has ended?**

A claim is not unbounded because it is severe.

It is unbounded when the theory cannot identify the state of affairs that discharges the exceptional claim.

Important distinction:

~~~text
large claim ≠ unbounded claim
old claim ≠ unbounded claim
ongoing claim ≠ unbounded claim

no principled stopping condition
        =
unboundedness
~~~

## Grievance branch: Unbounded revanchism

[Unbounded revanchism](../entries/unbounded-revanchism.md) specializes Unbounded claim.

~~~text
injury / humiliation / dispossession
        ↓
legitimate grievance
        ↓
bounded redress?
        ↓
claim remains open after redress
        ↓
past injury becomes renewable warrant
        ↓
restitution / punishment / exclusion /
status reversal / jurisdiction
~~~

The concept does **not** imply that historical claims, reparations, inherited consequences, or long-lived grievances are illegitimate.

The question is whether the grievance has **satisfaction conditions**.

## Closureless grievance and settlement refusal

[Closureless grievance](../entries/closureless-grievance.md) means the special normative claim lacks a recognized discharge state.

It should not be confused with memory.

~~~text
remember forever
      ≠
hold exceptional claim forever
~~~

[Settlement refusal](../entries/settlement-refusal.md) is a mechanism that can stabilize closurelessness by stripping actual or hypothetical settlements of terminating force.

This distinction protects the concept from an obvious abuse:

~~~text
rejecting a bad settlement
      ≠
settlement refusal
~~~

The latter requires either explicit denial that any settlement can discharge the claim or repeated displacement of previously stated closure conditions after fulfillment.

## Revanchist ratchet

[Revanchist ratchet](../entries/revanchist-ratchet.md) describes the dynamic where successful redress expands rather than reduces the remaining claim.

~~~text
redress
  ↓
validated grievance
  ↓
larger exceptional claim
  ↓
further redress
  ↓
larger exceptional claim
~~~

The anti-collapse rule is:

~~~text
new evidence of additional harm
      ≠
revanchist ratchet
~~~

The mechanism requires expansion because **redress itself** becomes warrant for more entitlement.

## Grievance-to-jurisdiction conversion

[Grievance-to-jurisdiction conversion](../entries/grievance-to-jurisdiction-conversion.md) is the negative-warrant analogue of credential-to-jurisdiction conversion.

~~~text
I was injured
      ↓
I have a claim to remedy
      ✕
not automatically:
I have standing authority over you
~~~

The concept lets Conceptarium preserve a strong theory of rights generated by injury while refusing to equate injury with sovereignty.

It may intersect [The Fallen humiliation hazard](../entries/fallen-humiliation-hazard.md), but humiliation is only a risk factor, not a sufficient cause.

## Status reversal

[Status-reversal entitlement](../entries/status-reversal-entitlement.md) is a possible endpoint:

~~~text
old domination was unjust
      ↓
therefore reverse domination is justice
~~~

The anti-domination alternative is:

~~~text
old domination was unjust
      ↓
remove arbitrary power
      ↓
do not create a new standing title to dominate
~~~

This distinction is load-bearing because status change, restitution, punishment, representation, and loss of illegitimate privilege are **not** automatically reverse domination.

## Inheritance

[Inherited revanchist warrant](../entries/inherited-revanchist-warrant.md) asks what survives when original parties die.

The cluster distinguishes:

~~~text
inherited consequence
inherited property claim
inherited treaty right
inherited memory
        ≠
inherited standing jurisdiction
~~~

Intergenerational injustice is real. The concept is specifically about whether **special authority itself** is transmitted without a bounded theory of liability and discharge.

## Relationship to Fallen constitutionalism

This cluster fits naturally beside [Fallen constitutionalism](../entries/fallen-constitutionalism.md).

One of the Fallen failure modes is:

~~~text
I rose
  ↓
my superiority is real
  ↓
therefore my model / prestige / achievement
confers title over others
~~~

The constitutional answer is not to deny ascent, intelligence, distinction, or internal grandeur.

It is to require a border between:

~~~text
capacity
   and
jurisdiction
~~~

This is why [Fallen dissident](../entries/fallen-dissident.md) matters: the anti-Fallen Fallen can retain obvious Fallen capacity while refusing solidarity with Fallen pretensions to rule.

## Anti-collapse rules

~~~text
expertise ≠ Infinite license
prestige ≠ sovereignty
long tenure ≠ temporal laundering
interdisciplinarity ≠ domain leakage
high removal threshold ≠ revocation asymmetry

grievance ≠ revanchism
large remedy ≠ unbounded claim
long-lived claim ≠ unbounded claim
historical memory ≠ inherited revanchist warrant
reparations ≠ status-reversal entitlement
rejecting an inadequate settlement ≠ settlement refusal

injury can generate rights
achievement can generate authority
neither automatically generates unbounded jurisdiction
~~~

## Research frontier

Future work should investigate:

- professional jurisdiction and credential expansion;
- technocratic legitimacy;
- emergency-power persistence;
- institutional revocation and sunset design;
- grievance and settlement theory;
- restorative versus retributive justice;
- intergenerational claims and liability;
- political revanchism;
- status-threat and humiliation pathways;
- how to formalize a **bounded warrant schema** with explicit scope, duration, target, remedy, inheritance, and revocation conditions.

The empirical research must remain separate from the conceptual architecture. The cluster provides distinctions; it does not pre-classify particular political movements or institutions as instances.
