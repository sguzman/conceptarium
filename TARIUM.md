# Tarium Meta Architecture

Conceptarium is the external meta-repository for a family of locally sovereign repository-scale knowledge systems.

This role is observational, not governing.

A Tarium repository preserves four distinct first-class layers:

```text
objective ledger
ontological structure
integration / analysis
phenomenology / confession
```

These layers are sovereign in epistemic function and integrated through explicit relations rather than silent conversion.

The governing repository-schema rule is:

> **Preserve fact, structure, analysis, and experience as distinct sovereign layers, then integrate them explicitly rather than collapsing them implicitly.**

Repository existence is itself constitutional rather than impulsive.

The governing sovereignty rule is:

> **Repository sovereignty is earned by ontological independence and structural pressure, not by enthusiasm alone.**

The candidate lifecycle is:

```text
candidate → incubating → approved → instantiated → active
```

with `merged`, `rejected`, and `dormant` preserving meaningful alternate outcomes.

Lifecycle maturity and physical repository existence are separate axes; an `approved` candidate may remain only a `stub` until it is deliberately instantiated.

The governing repository-ecology rule is:

> **Conceptarium may model another tarium without thereby governing it.**

The governing epistemic rule is:

> **No suppression of testimony. No immunity from audit.**

The governing knowledge boundary is:

```text
public predicate existence
        ≠
mirrored internal state
```

Conceptarium should remain a map of the archipelago rather than an empire over it.
