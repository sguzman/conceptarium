# Tarium Register

## Definition

**Tarium Register** is the default institutional prose realization of [`Tarium Procedure`](./tarium-procedure.md).

It is modern institutional-ontological prose with juridical discipline and an early-20th-century intellectual temperament: serene, articulate, declarative, scope-conscious, mildly elevated, and minimally ornamental.

Its authority should come from **structure rather than swagger**.

## Ancestry

The Register draws from several neighboring traditions without reproducing any of them wholesale.

### Scholarly monograph

Contributes confidence in explicit distinctions, conceptual apparatus, and careful qualification.

### Juridical prose

Contributes scope, jurisdiction, burdens, exceptions, non-implication, and the refusal to let one proposition silently imply another.

### Civil-service and commission prose

Contributes impersonal seriousness: the sense that a durable institution is calmly producing categories because the category is needed.

### Early-20th-century intellectual prose

Contributes composure, seriousness, and the assumption that subtle distinctions are ordinary obligations of educated thought.

The Register modernizes this ancestry by removing most ceremonial padding, archaism, conspicuous Latinism, and ornamental periodicity.

## Characteristic stance

The speaker behaves as though the subject deserves to be **classified correctly before anyone is permitted to argue about it**.

The prose therefore prefers institutional formulations:

- `This object represents ...`
- `This object exists to ...`
- `X does not imply Y.`
- `These cases must remain distinguishable.`
- `The present model does not decide ...`
- `These domains need not move together.`

The point is not impersonality for its own sake.

The point is to keep the ontology from becoming a concealed advocate.

## Default properties

Tarium Register should normally be:

- declarative rather than conversational;
- precise without technicality for prestige;
- willing to make distinctions aggressively;
- explicit about scope and non-implication;
- emotionally unflustered even when the object is morally explosive;
- authoritative through structure rather than personal dominance;
- mildly elevated without archaism;
- written for an intelligent reader rather than a reader being coached through every step;
- willing to model ambiguity explicitly;
- resistant to conclusions leaking backward into definitions;
- procedurally severe without suppressing testimony.

## Seriousness without sanctimony

The Register should be able to describe sex, domination, humiliation, violence, ideology, desire, status, or political accusation without adopting either internet combat language or ceremonial displays of moral credentials.

Its governing courtesy toward reality is:

> **The phenomenon will be permitted to exist long enough for us to describe it accurately. Judgment can come afterward.**

That does not make judgment weak.

It makes the sequence disciplined.

## Full-strength claims

The Register does not require euphemism.

A severe accusation can remain severe if the evidence warrants it.

The prose should instead make the accusation structurally auditable:

- who or what is the subject?
- in which domain?
- with what evidence?
- over what time range?
- under which definition?
- with which exceptions?
- what exactly follows?

The voice says, in effect:

> **State the accusation at full strength. Now specify what licenses it.**

## Sentence architecture

Prefer compact conceptual sentences over ceremonial setup.

Older institutional prose might say:

> It is necessary, if the matter is not to be obscured at the outset by a confusion of quite distinct considerations, to distinguish ...

Tarium Register prefers:

> Broad claims compress several domains that may not move together.

The intellectual temperament remains; the upholstery is removed.

## Scope markers

Useful constructions include:

- `does not imply`;
- `need not`;
- `is distinct from`;
- `should not be understood as`;
- `in this framework`;
- `for present purposes`;
- `the stronger claim would require`;
- `this object records ... without deciding ...`.

These constructions are not hedging when they prevent real category errors.

## Anti-patterns

### Swagger authority

The prose sounds certain because the speaker announces superiority rather than because the structure is explicit.

### Conversational diffusion

Excessive `I think`, `maybe`, `sort of`, or coaching language weakens an object that is already sufficiently defined.

### Academic credential display

The prose performs sophistication instead of increasing precision.

### Ornamental 1910 pastiche

Archaism, gentlemanly flourishes, and ceremonial verbosity become the point rather than the intellectual discipline.

### Moral throat-clearing

Every contentious description is preceded by ritual declarations of virtue rather than clear scope and evidence.

### False serenity

Calm tone is used to hide suppression, asymmetrical standards, or prejudged conclusions.

## Relation to Tarium Procedure

The Register is not itself the constitution.

[`Tarium Procedure`](./tarium-procedure.md) determines whether testimony has standing, whether uncertainty is represented, how claims are scoped, and whether epistemic burdens are applied symmetrically.

The Register is the recognizable prose surface produced when that Procedure is rendered in its default institutional voice.

```text
TARIUM PROCEDURE
    ↓ rendered as
TARIUM REGISTER
```

The Procedure should survive changes in style.
