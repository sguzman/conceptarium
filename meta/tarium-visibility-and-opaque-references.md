# Tarium Visibility and Opaque References

Repository ownership, lifecycle maturity, physical materialization, and visibility are separate dimensions.

The Tarium family must be able to acknowledge that an ontological object exists without requiring its substantive contents to be public.

The governing distinction is:

> **Knowledge that a private domain exists is not knowledge of the private domain's contents.**

This matters especially when Conceptarium is public while another Tarium, candidate province, evidence substrate, or artifact is private.

---

# Four independent questions

For any Tarium, candidate, province, or cross-repository object, ask separately:

1. **Canonical ownership** — where does the deepest ontology belong?
2. **Lifecycle state** — candidate, incubating, approved, instantiated, active, merged, rejected, or dormant?
3. **Materialization state** — stub, province, or repository?
4. **Visibility** — what may Conceptarium or the public know about the object and its substrate?

None of these dimensions determines the others.

A domain may therefore be:

```text
lifecycle: incubating
materialization: province
host: private repository
Conceptarium visibility: predicate-level only
```

without contradiction.

---

# Predicate-level public knowledge

When a private domain is represented in public Conceptarium, the public record may contain only the minimum architecture needed to situate it in the ecology.

Permitted predicate-level knowledge may include:

- that the domain exists;
- its working name or deliberately generic public label;
- its canonical object of study in coarse terms;
- its core question;
- its lifecycle and materialization state;
- the repository in which it is incubating, when disclosure of that repository is itself acceptable;
- its likely boundaries with other Tariums;
- the kinds of object classes and relations the domain is expected to require;
- its sovereignty assessment and promotion trigger;
- an explicit statement that substantive material is private.

Public predicate knowledge does **not** authorize Conceptarium to mirror:

- private source conversations;
- private evidence excerpts;
- personal facts discovered through the domain;
- concrete interaction rules whose publication would expose intimate information;
- trust histories;
- private vocabulary whose meaning is sensitive;
- psychological interpretations;
- model-specific incidents tied to private conversations;
- unpublished artifacts in the host repository.

---

# Opaque references

An **opaque reference** is a public statement that a private object or substrate exists and has a declared relation to the public architecture, while intentionally withholding its contents.

Example:

```text
Conceptarium candidate
    └─ incubates-in -> Salvatarium/private province
                       └─ substantive records withheld
```

An opaque reference is not a degraded copy of the private object. It is a different artifact with a narrower epistemic contract.

The public pointer should remain useful enough to preserve architecture while revealing no more than its visibility contract permits.

---

# Privacy is structural, not accidental

If a candidate's evidence substrate predictably contains intimate self-description, private relationships, trust conditions, interaction history, or other sensitive material, privacy should be declared at incubation time.

Do not build a public ontology first and attempt to redact it later.

The constitutional preference is:

> **Private substrate; explicit public pointer; no silent mirroring.**

---

# Visibility does not decide sovereignty

A private domain may eventually become a sovereign private Tarium.

Conversely, a public domain may remain only a province.

Secrecy is not evidence for sovereignty, and publicity is not evidence against it.

The Tarium Sovereignty Test remains unchanged: sovereignty must be earned through native objects, relations, questions, methods, subdomains, and structural pressure against the host.

---

# Secession of a private province

If a private incubating province later earns sovereignty:

```text
private province in host Tarium
    ↓
sovereignty test passes
    ↓
private sovereign Tarium
```

Conceptarium may update the public pointer from `incubates-in` to `repository-exists` without acquiring the private substrate.

The public record remains predicate-level unless the sovereign repository explicitly publishes a wider interface.

---

# Governing synthesis

```text
canonical ownership ≠ visibility
lifecycle ≠ visibility
materialization ≠ visibility
public pointer ≠ mirrored private state
```

The deepest rule is:

> **The Tarium ecology may know where a private ontology lives without possessing what it knows.**
