# Tarium Procedure

## Definition

**Tarium Procedure** is the cross-domain epistemic constitution underlying the Tarium style of analysis and institutional prose.

It grants claims, grievances, hypotheses, and subjective reports **full representational standing** while withholding automatic epistemic privilege from any of them.

Its core commitment is:

> **No suppression of testimony. No immunity from audit.**

A complementary formulation is:

> **Full standing. No epistemic privilege.**

## Governing temperament

The Procedure is severe about distinctions without becoming dismissive about experience.

It permits a complainant, theorist, witness, or internal voice to state the strongest version of a claim.

It then requires that claim to survive explicit scrutiny of:

- unit of analysis;
- domain;
- evidence;
- scope;
- counterfactual;
- exceptions;
- motive attribution;
- uncertainty;
- what the evidence actually licenses us to conclude.

The Procedure does not ask anger, fear, resentment, grief, or enthusiasm to disappear.

It requires them to become **legible**.

## Core sequence

```text
TESTIMONY / CLAIM / HYPOTHESIS
        ↓
grant standing
        ↓
identify object and unit of analysis
        ↓
separate observation from interpretation
        ↓
separate empirical claim from normative judgment
        ↓
represent uncertainty explicitly
        ↓
specify evidentiary scope
        ↓
test exceptions and counterfactuals
        ↓
audit generalization and motive attribution
        ↓
state only the conclusion licensed by the evidence
```

## Non-prejudgment

Definitions and schemas must not smuggle later conclusions into the ontology.

A data object may be designed to represent a disputed phenomenon without deciding in advance which theory of that phenomenon is correct.

The Procedure therefore prefers formulations such as:

- `X represents ...`;
- `X does not imply ...`;
- `This object exists to ...`;
- `These cases must remain distinguishable ...`.

The repository should be capable of hosting the argument before it decides the argument.

## Ambiguity

Ambiguity is not a defect to conceal.

When ambiguity materially affects interpretation, it should become first-class information:

- unresolved;
- contested;
- underdefined;
- confidence-bounded;
- branch-dependent;
- awaiting evidence.

The Procedure treats anonymous ambiguity as a modeling problem rather than an excuse for false certainty.

## Grievance and accusation

A grievance may be stated at full strength.

Its emotional or moral importance does not purchase permission to:

- collapse distinct categories;
- assign motives without evidence;
- convert a tendency into a universal;
- erase exceptions;
- infer total social structure from one domain;
- slander an opposing population through ontology design.

The implicit institutional promise is:

> **Your case will be heard. Your claims will also be audited.**

## Not moderation

Tarium Procedure is not a demand to split the difference between parties.

It is not tone-policing, compulsory charity, or an instruction to weaken accusations until nobody is offended.

A fully supported severe conclusion should remain severe.

The Procedure is neutral in a stronger sense: **the same epistemic burdens apply regardless of which side benefits from the result**.

## Procedural anti-domination

Procedure can itself become a domination technology if qualification requirements are used to make testimony disappear.

Therefore rigor and standing must be simultaneous.

```text
NO SUPPRESSION OF TESTIMONY
        +
NO IMMUNITY FROM AUDIT
```

Neither half is sufficient alone.

## Relation to Tarium Register

[`Tarium Register`](./tarium-register.md) is the default prose realization of this Procedure.

The Procedure is deeper than the Register.

The prose may become more terse, more public-facing, or more historically inflected without changing the governing epistemic rules.

## Failure modes

### Procedural laundering

Using demands for precision to indefinitely defer or suppress an inconvenient claim.

### Ontological prejudgment

Encoding the preferred conclusion into definitions, schemas, or categories before evidence is evaluated.

### Symmetry theater

Pretending to apply one procedure to all sides while quietly changing evidentiary burdens by constituency.

### Credential substitution

Treating institutional status as a substitute for evidence.

### Grievance privilege

Treating genuine injury as automatic authorization for unsupported generalization.

### Sterile neutrality

Removing so much substantive language that the procedure can no longer represent what people are actually claiming.
