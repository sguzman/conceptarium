- **Materialization:** province

- **Sovereignty:** not yet earned

Garments, dress, costume, bodily presentation, textile form, and the operations clothing performs on a human body.

## Native pressure

Drape, silhouette, concealment, exposure, movement, texture, social signaling, sex differentiation, eroticism, status, identity, costume, genealogy, construction.

## Promotion trigger

Reconsider sovereignty if garment construction, textile systems, dress history, costume ontology, industry structure, specialized assets/tooling, and multiple independent subdomains grow beyond what Designarium can represent honestly.
