# Human Biology — merged Tarium candidate

- **Lifecycle:** merged
- **Disposition:** Somatarium

Human biology does not receive a separate Tarium because Somatarium already provides the appropriate deeper ontology: the human organism can be represented alongside personal measurements, interventions, symptoms, hypotheses, and first-person embodied phenomenology while preserving those epistemic layers separately.

General comparative biology can project to or from Biotarium without changing Somatarium's canonical ownership of the embodied human domain.
