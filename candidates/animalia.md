# Animalia — merged Tarium candidate

- **Lifecycle:** merged
- **Disposition:** Biotarium

Animalia was considered as a sovereign repository for animals, phylogeny, speciation, anatomy, behavior, and evolution.

The candidate was merged because animal-only sovereignty would fragment a deeper nonhuman-life ontology and privilege animals over plants, fungi, protists, bacteria, and archaea.

The intellectual substrate is preserved as a major Biotarium province.

This is a positive ontological conclusion, not discarded work.
