# Somatarium

## Predicate identity

- **Repository:** `sguzman/somatarium`
- **Family status:** core
- **Role:** private embodied-domain repository
- **State:** active / instantiated

## Coarse purpose

Somatarium ontologizes Salvador's body and embodied organism while preserving several epistemic projections as first-class and non-interchangeable.

Its domain includes, at coarse level:

- anatomy and physiology;
- nervous system and cognition where embodied mechanisms are relevant;
- medicine, symptoms, measurements, medications, sleep, and bodily interventions;
- general human biology where required to understand the personal organism;
- hypotheses and interpretations about bodily state;
- first-person bodily and mental phenomenology.

Its constitutional distinction is:

```text
known / measured / sourced bodily fact
        ≠
model or interpretation of the body
        ≠
first-person bodily phenomenology
```

Phenomenology is first-class without being promoted to absolute or external truth.

## Tarium Repository Schema projection

Somatarium is an especially clear application of the four-layer Tarium Repository Schema:

- **Objective ledger:** measurements, diagnoses where established, test results, medication records, anatomy, physiology, source-backed medical or biological facts.
- **Ontological structure:** body systems, organs, tissues, processes, symptoms, interventions, states, relations, and temporal changes.
- **Integration and analysis:** causal hypotheses, competing interpretations, longitudinal synthesis, uncertainty, and explanatory models.
- **Phenomenological / confessional:** pain, fatigue, hunger, embodiment, bodily self-perception, mental/bodily experience, subjective response to intervention, and personal interpretation.

No one layer may silently impersonate another.

## Boundary with Salvatarium

Somatarium owns the embodied organism and bodily state.

Salvatarium owns Salvador as a broader self-ontological object: identities, agents, values, desires, fears, contradictions, self-concept, confessional self-model, and related psychological/personal structures.

The two repositories may project into one another without either becoming a mirror of the other.

## Boundary with Biotarium candidate

General nonhuman biology belongs to the Biotarium candidate domain.

Human biology may legitimately appear in Somatarium because the human organism is not merely an abstract species object here: it is also the substrate of measurements, interventions, symptoms, and first-person embodied experience.

Comparative or general biological concepts may later be linked across the two domains explicitly.

## Conceptarium knowledge boundary

Conceptarium may know that Somatarium exists, its coarse purpose, its family role, and any interfaces Somatarium deliberately publishes.

Conceptarium should not mirror Somatarium's private health records, bodily measurements, symptoms, hypotheses, medical artifacts, or current internal conclusions.

## Authority boundary

Somatarium is authoritative over its own local ontology, artifacts, epistemic distinctions, and internal state.

Conceptarium's representation of Somatarium is an external predicate-level projection only.
