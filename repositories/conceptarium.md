# Conceptarium

## Predicate identity

- **Repository:** `sguzman/conceptarium`
- **Family status:** core Tarium member
- **Role:** meta-repository
- **Broad domain:** concepts, cross-domain ontology, and external theorization of the Tarium ecology

## External classification

Conceptarium is the repository in which concepts are canonically preserved and materialized, and the repository from which cross-Tarium structural patterns may be theorized.

Its meta role is observational rather than sovereign.

It may describe the family, propose classifications, and preserve shared procedures without thereby acquiring authority over the internal ontologies of other repositories.

## Conceptarium knowledge boundary

Conceptarium may know that other repositories exist, what broad domains they claim, and what relations or interfaces they explicitly publish.

It should not mirror their internal artifact inventories, live object state, private records, or local conclusions by default.

## Authority boundary

Conceptarium is authoritative for its own ontology and for its own external descriptions of the repository ecology.

It is not authoritative for Politicarium, Linguarium, Salvatarium, Dramatarium, Projectarium, or Software Philosophy.

See [`docs/TARIUM_ECOLOGY.md`](../docs/TARIUM_ECOLOGY.md).
