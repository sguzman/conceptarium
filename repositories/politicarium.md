# Politicarium

## Predicate identity

- **Repository:** `sguzman/politicarium`
- **Family status:** core Tarium member
- **Role:** domain repository
- **Broad domain:** politics, political theory, confession, arguments, and audits

## External classification

Politicarium is the Tarium repository dedicated to political theory and political analysis, including confessional, argumentative, and audit-oriented work.

Conceptarium may recognize that broad role and theorize cross-repository patterns involving it.

It does not inherit Politicarium's internal political ontology merely by describing Politicarium from outside.

## Conceptarium knowledge boundary

Conceptarium records predicate-level existence and coarse domain purpose only.

Detailed political objects, claims, audits, arguments, persons, and conclusions remain local to Politicarium unless Politicarium explicitly publishes an interface or Conceptarium independently develops a concept that belongs here on its own merits.

## Authority boundary

Politicarium is authoritative for its own political ontology and artifacts.

Conceptarium's classification of Politicarium is an external model, not a governing schema.
