# Internal Inquisition

## Definition

The **Internal Inquisition** is introspection converted into a permanent prosecutorial institution.

~~~text
everything is examinable
        ↓ distorted into
everything is suspicious
        ↓ distorted into
everything must justify its existence
        ↓
permanent internal prosecution
~~~

The failure is not examination itself.

It is the destruction of neutral standing and closure.

## Core properties

- universal suspicion;
- impossible burdens of proof;
- repeated reopening without new evidence;
- moralization of ordinary pleasure;
- punishment mistaken for rigor;
- no rehabilitation;
- no distinction between impulse and action;
- no stable right to remain ordinary.

## Relation to universal analytical jurisdiction

The confessional constitution says everything may in principle be examined.

The Internal Inquisition corrupts this into:

> Everything must continuously defend itself.

Those are not equivalent.

## Relation to no torture authority

[No torture authority](./no-torture-authority.md) is a direct constitutional barrier.

The ability to investigate never creates unlimited punitive jurisdiction.

## Relation to containment

A dangerous impulse can be firmly contained without turning the entire psyche into a prosecutorial state.

That distinction is preserved by [Containment without cruelty](./containment-without-cruelty.md).

## Failure pattern

~~~text
uncertainty
→ suspicion
→ prosecution
→ punishment
→ punishment creates more suspicious evidence
→ renewed prosecution
~~~

The system becomes self-reinforcing.
