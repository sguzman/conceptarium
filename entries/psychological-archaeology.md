# Psychological archaeology

## Definition

**Psychological archaeology** reconstructs how old conclusions, rules, fears, and interpretations were formed and why they still affect the present.

~~~text
present reaction / prohibition / assumption
        ↓
trace backward
        ↓
earlier experience
        ↓
earlier interpretation
        ↓
rule or proposition formed
        ↓
rule persists after context changes
~~~

The method treats present psychology as historically layered rather than temporally flat.

## Problem pressure

A present rule can feel self-evident because the event that produced it has disappeared from active memory.

Psychological archaeology asks:

- When did this belief first become necessary?
- What did the earlier self know?
- What danger was being modeled?
- What function did the rule serve?
- Which conditions have changed?
- Which parts of the original information remain valid?

## Archaeology is not debunking

Discovering where a belief came from does not automatically invalidate it.

~~~text
historical origin
        ≠
present falsity
~~~

Provenance creates evidence for review, not a guilty verdict.

## Archaeological objects

The method can recover:

- mental notes;
- inherited internal rules;
- stale propositions;
- old prohibitions;
- fear associations;
- identity conclusions;
- defensive strategies;
- obsolete predictions.

## Relation to confessional epistemology

[Confessional epistemology](./confessional-epistemology.md) supplies disclosure, interrogation, recursive scrutiny, and procedural review.

Psychological archaeology specializes that machinery toward **temporal provenance**.

## Relation to psychological refactoring

The goal is not merely excavation.

Once a legacy rule is understood, [Psychological refactoring](./psychological-refactoring.md) can preserve valid information while changing obsolete implementation.

## Failure modes

- origin fallacy;
- false-memory confidence;
- over-narrativization;
- assuming every present pattern has one decisive origin;
- using childhood explanation to erase present agency.
