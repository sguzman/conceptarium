# Sovereignty over abundance

## Definition

**Sovereignty over abundance** is the condition in which access to desirable goods does not automatically commandeer behavior. The person can possess, approach, or decline abundance without having their action schedule dictated by whatever reward is most immediate.

The normative center is self-government: abundance is genuinely empowering only if the chooser remains capable of saying yes, no, later, enough, or not this.

The compact originating definition was:

> The ability to remain self-governing despite unrestricted access to pleasurable or desirable goods.

## Problem pressure

Post-scarcity is often imagined as liberation through unrestricted access. The missing question is whether access can become another form of control when reward systems are strong enough to capture attention and behavior. If scarcity disappears but agency collapses into compulsive consumption, material abundance has not produced full sovereignty.

## Core model / mechanism

```text
abundant option
      ↓
desire registered
      ↓
internal priorities remain available
      ↓
choice among consume / defer / abstain
      ↓
behavior remains self-authored
```

## Claims and implications

Sovereignty over abundance is stronger than mere abstinence. Someone sovereign can sometimes indulge heavily and still remain governed if the indulgence is genuinely chosen and bounded. The target is not ascetic purity but non-capture.

## Examples

Keeping unlimited entertainment available without losing the ability to work or sleep; enjoying rich food while retaining the ability to stop; possessing wealth without allowing consumption opportunities to set every priority.

## Non-examples

Forced deprivation; total lack of desire; abstention driven entirely by fear of punishment; compulsive restriction that itself overrides reflective goals.

## Boundaries and failure modes

Avoid defining sovereignty as perfect rational control. Human action is embodied, habitual, social, and partly automatic. The concept asks whether meaningful self-direction survives, not whether desire disappears.

## Operationalization / evidence

Look for stable pursuit of higher-order priorities under high access, ability to interrupt consumption, flexible rather than rigid restraint, and recovery after indulgence. Self-report alone is insufficient.

## Relations

**Abundance discipline** is a capacity that helps produce sovereignty; the **internalized governor** is the regulatory architecture; **constraint substitution** can create supportive scaffolding without making scarcity itself the sovereign.
