# Physical pooling / legal individuation

## Definition

**Physical pooling / legal individuation** separates:

- economic pooling — money or assets are managed together;
- legal/accounting individuation — claims over that pool remain attached to identifiable parties.

Fungibility makes physical units interchangeable. It does not make claims impossible to individuate.

## Problem pressure

The source repeatedly encountered “money is fungible” as an intuitive reason taxpayer provenance could not survive a general fund.

The distinction shows that this is an engineering observation, not a normative conclusion. Banking, brokerage, insurance, and pooled investment systems maintain individualized claims over fungible assets.

## Core model

~~~text
many contributors
       ↓
one pooled treasury
       ↓
shared liquidity

while simultaneously:

contributor A → legal share / attribution
contributor B → legal share / attribution
contributor C → legal share / attribution
~~~

## Claims and implications

The distinction does not prove taxpayer claims **should** survive. It proves only that fungibility is insufficient to explain why they cannot.

This moves the debate from technical impossibility to institutional choice.

## Examples

### Clean example

A bank pools deposits operationally while maintaining each depositor’s balance.

### Borderline example

A treasury retains only contributor classes rather than individual claims.

### Cross-domain example

A pension fund pools investments while preserving beneficiary entitlements.

## Non-examples

Keeping every taxpayer’s dollars in a separate account is not required. Legal individuation also does not imply a right to withdraw a tax payment on demand.

## Boundaries and failure modes

Analogy to banks can overreach because taxation, deposits, trusts, and investments create different legal relationships. The concept establishes separability, not identity.

## Operationalization / evidence

Inspect whether the system can represent contributor-specific shares after pooling, whether transformations preserve them, and whether the claims have legal consequences or are merely informational labels.

## Relations

The distinction supports **Fiscal provenance**, enables **Treasury fungibility / taxpayer sovereignty**, and contrasts with **Claim extinction**.
