# Self-exemption

## Definition

**Self-exemption** occurs when analytical tools are applied outward but not inward with comparable seriousness.

~~~text
history / institutions / ideology / other people
        ↓
high-resolution analysis

own motives / assumptions / preferred identity
        ↓
presumed obvious or protected
~~~

The asymmetry creates a privileged observer who is not required to explain themselves.

## Why it matters

A person can become excellent at detecting:

- incentives;
- domination;
- ideology;
- status;
- self-interest;
- institutional capture;

while assuming that their own judgments arrive without equivalent causal structure.

Self-exemption makes analytical sophistication compatible with deep blind spots.

## Relation to epistemic immunity

Self-exemption can produce [Epistemic immunity](./epistemic-immunity.md).

The difference is:

- **self-exemption** describes the asymmetry of application;
- **epistemic immunity** describes protected status granted to a claim or internal authority.

## Countermeasures

- recursive introspection;
- confession;
- role reversal;
- adversarial hypotheses;
- provenance tracing;
- procedural self-analysis.

## Non-examples

Self-exemption is not merely failing to analyze every thought.

No one can continuously inspect everything.

The failure appears when **role or identity itself is treated as sufficient reason for exemption**.
