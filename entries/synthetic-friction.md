# Synthetic friction

## Definition

**Synthetic friction** is intentionally designed resistance inserted where technology or abundance would otherwise make gratification immediate. It can slow, cap, ration, sequence, require effort, hide options, impose cooldowns, or create commitment costs.

Its purpose is not necessarily deprivation. It is to restore functions that natural scarcity used to provide: pacing, salience, discipline, anticipation, attention, or credible effort.

The compact originating definition was:

> Deliberately introduced difficulty, limitation, or delay intended to preserve discipline, meaning, attention, or status in an otherwise frictionless environment.

## Problem pressure

A frictionless world can remove not only inconvenience but also the behavioral structure produced by inconvenience. The design problem becomes whether some lost functions should be rebuilt deliberately rather than waiting for scarcity to return.

## Core model / mechanism

```text
natural friction removed
        ↓
useful regulatory function disappears
        ↓
identify lost function
        ↓
design chosen / institutional resistance
        ↓
synthetic friction restores function
```

## Claims and implications

Good synthetic friction should be function-specific. It is not “make everything harder.” The design question is what friction accomplishes, for whom, and with what exit rights.

## Examples

App time limits; delayed purchase confirmation; device-free rooms; deliberately slow educational sequencing; reservation systems that prevent constant impulsive access; voluntary fasting schedules.

## Non-examples

Random lag; bureaucratic incompetence; punitive scarcity imposed without purpose; artificial shortages created purely to raise prices.

## Boundaries and failure modes

Synthetic friction can become paternalistic, manipulative, or rent-seeking. It can also be gamed or become mere theater. The anti-domination question is whether users understand, choose, contest, or exit the constraint.

## Operationalization / evidence

Evaluate whether the introduced friction improves the target outcome—attention, health, pacing, retention, deliberation—relative to a frictionless baseline, and measure user autonomy and circumvention behavior.

## Relations

Synthetic friction is an implementation of **constraint substitution**, can support **abundance discipline**, and may be demanded or valorized because of the **friction premium**.
