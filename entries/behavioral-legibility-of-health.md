# Behavioral legibility of health

## Definition

**Behavioral legibility of health** is the extent to which observers can plausibly read health outcomes as traces of behavior after major material, environmental, and access constraints have been reduced.

The word **legibility** is intentionally dangerous here: an outcome can become *more interpretable* without becoming perfectly attributable.

The compact originating definition was:

> The degree to which health outcomes appear interpretable as consequences of personal behavior because environmental and material constraints have been reduced.

## Problem pressure

In the post-scarcity thought experiment, equalized medicine and resources seemed to strip away some obvious external explanations for health differences. That creates pressure to read the body as evidence of self-government. The concept is needed both to describe that shift and to warn against overreading it.

## Core model / mechanism

```text
external constraint variance falls
        ↓
behavioral contribution becomes relatively more visible
        ↓
health state appears more interpretable
        ↓
social attribution to self-regulation increases
```

## Claims and implications

The central claim is comparative: behavior may become *more legible* as one contributor when other causes are reduced. It never becomes fully legible because genetics, stochastic disease, hidden exposure, developmental history, and measurement error remain.

## Examples

Two people with genuinely similar food access, healthcare, housing, and working conditions but long histories of different voluntary consumption; longitudinal adherence differences under equal treatment access.

## Non-examples

Reading present-day obesity, disability, or chronic illness directly as character; assuming a visible body reveals exact behavior; ignoring medication effects, disease, poverty, genetics, or trauma.

## Boundaries and failure modes

This term must not become a moral shortcut. **Legibility is not guilt.** A more behaviorally interpretable environment still contains enormous causal opacity. Bodies are noisy records.

## Operationalization / evidence

Estimate the fraction of outcome variance attributable to measured behaviors after controlling for material, medical, environmental, genetic, and stochastic factors. Track uncertainty explicitly.

## Relations

It is adjacent to **institutional legibility** but concerns causal interpretation of embodied outcomes rather than administrative readability. **Embodied history** supports the intuition, while **longevity sorting** is one hypothesized domain where it might increase.
