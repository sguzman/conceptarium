# Moral category collapse

## Definition

The error of treating moral equality as if it implied identical social, psychological, developmental, or epistemic position.

The collapse mistakes one axis of equality for equivalence across all descriptive and institutional axes.

## Problem pressure

The thread needed to preserve equal human worth without erasing differences in formation, capacity, optionality, dependency, responsibility, and hazard.

The broader thread was trying to preserve exceptional intellectual capacity without either converting it into jurisdiction over others or demanding that the person erase the ego that carries the capacity.

## Core model

~~~text
equal moral worth
        ↓ illicit inference
identical position / capacity / risk
        ↓
moral category collapse
~~~

## Claims and implications

Anti-hierarchy arguments can become analytically destructive when they require pretending that consequential differences do not exist.

This entry belongs to the larger architecture of **psychic sovereignty, epistemic submission, and social non-domination**.

## Examples

### Clean example

Two people are treated as having identical exit options because acknowledging unequal mobility is feared to imply unequal human worth.

### Borderline example

A case in which the same outward condition exists but exit capacity, motive, actual power, or scope of authority differs.

## Non-examples

Insisting that different capacities do not change basic rights or human dignity.

## Boundaries and failure modes

The concept can be abused to smuggle hierarchy back into moral worth. Descriptive non-equivalence must remain explicitly separated from claims of superior personhood.

Do not treat Fallen/Village vocabulary as a biological caste or proof of moral superiority.

## Operationalization / evidence

Track the concrete variables relevant to the concept: actual exit options, retained capacities, domain-specific authority, response to correction, consent, contestability, and whether a representational or status advantage is converted into claims over others.
