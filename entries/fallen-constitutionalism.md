# Fallen constitutionalism

## Definition

The broader doctrine implied by this thread: Fallen capacities are not to be denied or purified away; instead, their movement into real power must be bounded by structures that do not depend on the purity, humility, or innocence of the person wielding them.

The framework is constitutional because it distrusts character-based guarantees. Goodness is welcome but not load-bearing.

## Problem pressure

The thread arrived at a constitutional answer to the problem of dangerous excellence: do not ask a powerful ego to become harmless; build rules around the points where its capacities become power over others.

The broader thread was testing whether Fallen danger is better solved by self-erasure or by a constitutional architecture that survives pride, status pressure, and human fallibility.

## Core model

~~~text
retain Fallen capacity
 abstraction / ambition / authorship / mobility
        ↓
accept permanent non-innocence
        ↓
psychic sovereignty + epistemic submission
        ↓
constitutionalized border crossing
        ↓
social non-domination
~~~

## Claims and implications

Strong individuality and excellence can coexist with external checks, consent, contestability, and model corrigibility.

This entry belongs to the larger architecture of **psychic sovereignty, epistemic submission, and social non-domination**.

## Examples

### Clean example

A brilliant institution-builder is permitted wide creative discretion in private design but cannot impose the design without procedures that preserve affected persons’ challenge and exit.

### Borderline example

A case that contains one element of the pattern—humiliation, correction, asceticism, ambition, or prestige—without the full mechanism required by the concept.

## Non-examples

A meritocratic doctrine claiming that the most capable should rule because they are most capable.

## Boundaries and failure modes

Constitutionalism can become formal theater if power can bypass its borders. The framework requires practical enforcement, not merely ethical aspiration.

The concept should not be used to infer violence, domination, or pathology from pride, humiliation, or ambition alone.

## Operationalization / evidence

For humiliation and status mechanisms, trace mediating conditions rather than relying on a single cause. For constitutional principles, inspect whether checks remain effective when the actor is trusted, admired, angry, or wrong. For corrigibility, observe behavior under high-identity-cost correction rather than only trivial mistakes.
