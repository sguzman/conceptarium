# Subpoena power of introspection

## Definition

**Subpoena power of introspection** is the operational right to call any internal claim into examination.

A belief, motive, memory, identity, or internal authority may be asked:

- What are you claiming?
- What evidence supports you?
- Where did you come from?
- What function do you serve?
- What would change your conclusion?
- What jurisdiction are you claiming?

## Jurisdiction before subpoena

The metaphor depends on [Analytical jurisdiction](./analytical-jurisdiction.md).

A subpoena is legitimate because the object lies inside the domain of inquiry, not because it is already suspected of wrongdoing.

~~~text
may be called to testify
        ≠
is guilty
~~~

## Why preserve the metaphor?

It captures a strong rule without demanding constant examination.

A court can possess subpoena power without subpoenaing every person.

Likewise introspection can preserve universal examinability while using attention selectively.

## Constitutional constraint

Subpoena power is paired with [No torture authority](./no-torture-authority.md).

The ability to compel examination does not confer permission to humiliate, erase, or psychologically brutalize the object being examined.

## Relation to confessional epistemology

Confessional epistemology uses subpoena power to prevent hidden protected zones in the self-model.

It is especially useful when:

- an old rule still governs behavior;
- a motive seems unquestionable;
- one evaluator claims neutrality;
- a memory has become constitutional law.
