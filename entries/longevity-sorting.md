# Longevity sorting

## Definition

**Longevity sorting** is a hypothesis about lifespan divergence after major material and medical access inequalities are reduced. If acute consequences of indulgence can be treated but cumulative behavior still matters, differences in maintenance, risk, intake, sleep, exercise, or adherence could become relatively more important sources of lifespan variation.

The term names a possible sorting mechanism, not a claim that current longevity differences are primarily self-caused.

The compact originating definition was:

> Differential lifespan produced by cumulative differences in self-regulation, risk, indulgence, or bodily maintenance under conditions where medical access is broadly equalized.

## Problem pressure

The thought experiment imagined medicine becoming broadly available enough to counter many consequences of overindulgence. The remaining question was whether long-run survival would then increasingly reveal differential self-regulation: some people repeatedly spending biological slack faster than others.

## Core model / mechanism

```text
medical/material access broadly equalized
        ↓
acute harms increasingly repairable
        ↓
behavioral exposure still accumulates
        ↓
different maintenance / risk trajectories
        ↓
lifespan divergence
```

## Claims and implications

This is explicitly conditional. It predicts a change in the *composition* of longevity inequality only if access inequalities and many environmental hazards are substantially reduced. It does not justify interpreting present-day lifespan as a moral score.

## Examples

A hypothetical post-scarcity population with equal preventive medicine but different chronic consumption/risk patterns; equal access to repair technologies with divergent adherence to maintenance protocols.

## Non-examples

Current mortality gaps driven by poverty, pollution, healthcare access, occupational danger, racism, violence, or unequal housing; genetic disease differences independent of behavior.

## Boundaries and failure modes

This concept is especially vulnerable to victim-blaming and false causal legibility. Biology, luck, genetics, social stress, and hidden environmental exposure remain powerful even under abundance. “Sorting” is descriptive, not deservedness.

## Operationalization / evidence

A serious test would require unusually strong controls or genuine equalization of healthcare, environment, and material security, followed longitudinally. Decompose genetic, stochastic, social, and behavioral variance rather than attributing residual outcomes to character.

## Relations

Longevity sorting is an **instance of abundance sorting**. It becomes socially dangerous when combined with excessive **behavioral legibility of health**, while **embodied history** describes one reason bodies may appear to record cumulative trajectories.
