# Constraint substitution

## Definition

**Constraint substitution** occurs when a naturally imposed limit disappears and a deliberately chosen or designed limit takes over some of its former function.

The new constraint may mimic the old one superficially, but its legitimacy, flexibility, and purpose can differ because it is authored rather than simply suffered.

The compact originating definition was:

> The replacement of naturally occurring external constraints with voluntarily chosen or institutionally designed constraints.

## Problem pressure

Post-scarcity removes constraints that were oppressive and constraints that were quietly useful at the same time. The design problem is not whether to restore scarcity wholesale, but whether specific lost regulatory functions should receive substitutes.

## Core model / mechanism

```text
natural constraint removed
        ↓
lost function identified
        ↓
voluntary / institutional substitute designed
        ↓
behavioral structure restored without original scarcity
```

## Claims and implications

Constraint substitution is broader than synthetic friction. A substitute can be a rule, norm, quota, schedule, ritual, architectural boundary, or institutional procedure.

## Examples

Replacing limited food access with a chosen meal schedule; replacing travel difficulty with training requirements for an expedition; replacing limited media availability with attention budgets or device-free periods.

## Non-examples

Recreating scarcity solely for monopoly rents; a constraint that has no relation to a lost function; accidental inconvenience.

## Boundaries and failure modes

Substituted constraints can become domination. The framework should ask who authors them, who benefits, whether they are transparent, and whether exit or appeal exists.

## Operationalization / evidence

Identify a removed natural constraint, specify the function it supplied, then test whether the substitute restores that function with lower collateral cost.

## Relations

Constraint substitution produces many forms of **synthetic friction**, can support **abundance discipline** and **sovereignty over abundance**, and becomes a central design instrument of **abundance politics**.
