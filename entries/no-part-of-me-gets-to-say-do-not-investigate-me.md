# No part of me gets to say: do not investigate me

## Definition

**No part of me gets to say: do not investigate me** is a constitutional rule for inward inquiry.

It denies permanent epistemic immunity to:

- beliefs;
- identities;
- motives;
- memories;
- interpretations;
- moral authorities;
- self-protective narratives;
- the investigator;
- the tribunal;
- the procedure itself.

The phrase is stronger than ordinary openness to self-reflection.

It says that **the right to be examined is universal across the internal polity**.

## Problem pressure

A theory of radical self-analysis can fail if one internal authority quietly becomes sovereign.

~~~text
"Question everything."
        ↓
except:
the rule saying question everything
the identity authorizing the rule
the investigator applying it
the tribunal issuing verdicts
~~~

Once that exemption exists, the method can reproduce the very immunity it claims to oppose.

The principle closes that loophole.

## Constitutional rather than punitive

The rule concerns **jurisdiction**, not punishment.

~~~text
may be investigated
        ≠
must be condemned
        ≠
may be humiliated
~~~

This distinction is essential.

Universal examinability is paired elsewhere with **No torture authority** and **containment without cruelty**.

The point is to deny immunity, not to create an unlimited prosecution state.

## Why the investigator is included

The most dangerous exemption is often the reflective authority itself.

The investigator may claim:

> “I am the one finding bias, therefore I am not biased.”

The principle rejects that inference.

The investigator is inside the same epistemic jurisdiction as everything else.

This makes [Investigating the investigator](./investigating-the-investigator.md) not an optional flourish but a constitutional consequence.

## Contestability

The rule is a psychological version of contestability.

Any internal authority may be asked:

- Why should I believe you?
- What evidence supports this?
- What standard are you using?
- What would change your conclusion?
- What do you gain from this judgment?
- Are you applying the same rules elsewhere?

No authority is invalid merely because it is questioned.

Questionability is a condition of legitimate authority, not its destruction.

## Relation to epistemic immunity

**Epistemic immunity** is the protected status of a claim or voice beyond challenge.

This principle directly prevents such immunity.

~~~text
authority
      +
no right to permanent exemption
      ↓
contestable internal order
~~~

## Relation to internal tribunal

The [Internal tribunal](./internal-tribunal.md) remains able to judge claims.

But it cannot create a constitutional class of unquestionable judges.

The tribunal itself remains reviewable through procedural self-analysis.

## Relation to no diplomatic immunity for the self

The queued phrase **No diplomatic immunity for the self** generalizes the same doctrine:

> intellectual tools applied to the world must also be applicable to oneself.

The current phrase is more recursive because it explicitly includes internal authorities.

## Examples

A person questions an identity claim, then applies the same scrutiny to the part insisting that the identity claim must be false.

Someone investigates a recurring motive, then investigates why the tribunal is so invested in a punitive interpretation.

A person permits a favored moral self-story to be tested by the same evidence rules used against disfavored impulses.

## Non-examples

The principle is not:

- “every thought is suspicious”;
- “nothing can be trusted”;
- “all internal voices are equal”;
- endless compulsory analysis;
- permission for cruelty;
- rejection of stable identity.

It is a rule against **permanent exemption**.

## Boundaries and failure modes

### Infinite jurisdiction

The principle does not require constant active investigation of every mental event.

### Suspicion totalization

Contestability should not become the assumption that every internal authority is corrupt.

### Torture drift

The right to question cannot become the right to psychologically brutalize.

### Anti-commitment misuse

A belief can remain stable and deeply held while still being in principle revisable.

## Operationalization

The principle is active when:

- favored identities can be questioned;
- disfavored identities can present evidence;
- the investigator's standards can be audited;
- tribunal procedures can be revised;
- no conclusion is protected solely by role;
- stopping inquiry remains provisional rather than metaphysically final.

## Relations

The principle **prevents epistemic immunity**.

It **constrains the internal tribunal** by keeping judges reviewable.

It **enables recursive introspection** by making the investigator available as an object.

It overlaps with the queued doctrine **No diplomatic immunity for the self**.
