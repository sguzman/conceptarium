# Psychic imperialism

## Definition

The unrestricted incorporation of reality into one’s private conceptual world. Anything may be modeled, criticized, subordinated to theory, mythologized, recombined, or intellectually conquered.

Psychic imperialism is intentionally provocative: the imperial scope is confined to representation unless and until a separate justified crossing occurs.

## Problem pressure

The thread wanted to preserve the system-builder’s expansive internal appetite rather than pathologizing every act of conceptual annexation.

The broader thread was trying to preserve exceptional intellectual capacity without either converting it into jurisdiction over others or demanding that the person erase the ego that carries the capacity.

## Core model

~~~text
world / persons / institutions
        ↓ private representation
model / myth / critique / recombination
        ↓
unrestricted interior conquest
        ✕ no automatic external title
~~~

## Claims and implications

The architecture allows maximal conceptual freedom while relocating ethics to the border between representation and action.

This entry belongs to the larger architecture of **psychic sovereignty, epistemic submission, and social non-domination**.

## Examples

### Clean example

A thinker privately interprets every social institution through a personal theoretical framework without claiming that anyone else must accept or live by it.

### Borderline example

A case in which similar surface behavior exists but the underlying status, optionality, or jurisdictional structure differs.

## Non-examples

Using a private model to manipulate another person without consent.

## Boundaries and failure modes

Representation can become power through publication, profiling, surveillance, or automated decision systems. Those cases must be evaluated as border crossings rather than treated as purely psychic.

Do not treat Fallen/Village vocabulary as a biological caste or proof of moral superiority.

## Operationalization / evidence

Where empirical, examine actual options, observable status dynamics, reflexive behavior, institutional authority, and whether prestige or representation is converted into external constraint. Where normative, ask whether the rule preserves equal standing without falsifying real asymmetries.
