# Fiscal standing

## Definition

**Fiscal standing** is the proposed enforcement right by which a taxpayer can personally challenge a violation tied to the taxpayer’s own attributable contribution.

The distinctive claim is that the amount need not be economically large. If the legal system recognizes the residual right, a tiny attributable violation can still be an injury.

Standing transforms taxpayer rights from declarative principles into claims with a holder and remedy.

## Problem pressure

The source recognized a failure mode: rights can be beautifully stated and then made unenforceable except by legislatures or inspectors general.

It therefore insisted that if only a few cents of a contribution is illegally routed somewhere the taxpayer has a legal right to exclude, **the taxpayer has been injured**.

## Core model

~~~text
recognized residual fiscal right
        ↓
traceable attributable contribution
        ↓
violation
        ↓
personal injury
        ↓
standing → review → remedy
~~~

## Claims and implications

The concept is a normative proposal, not a description of current standing doctrine. It is strongest where a legal system already creates contributor-specific rights. Provenance alone does not automatically imply judicial standing.

## Examples

### Clean example

A statute forbids a taxpayer’s discretionary allocation from funding a specified program. The ledger shows $0.03 was routed there anyway. The taxpayer can seek correction.

### Borderline example

A taxpayer objects to a general budget choice but cannot identify any violated contributor-specific right.

## Non-examples

General disagreement with taxation or spending is not by itself fiscal standing. Standing is also not equivalent to winning the case.

## Boundaries and failure modes

Mass individualized standing can create litigation and administrative load. Systems may need standardized remedies, aggregation, ombuds processes, or automated correction.

The concept must avoid manufacturing artificial attribution solely to create litigation hooks.

## Operationalization / evidence

Define the protected right, attributable amount, challenged transformation, forum, and remedy. Test whether the process remains accessible for small-value claims.
