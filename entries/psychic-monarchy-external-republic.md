# Psychic monarchy, external republic

## Definition

A compact formulation of the architecture developed here: internally, the ego may rule extravagantly; externally, other persons remain sovereign and no internal grandeur automatically crosses the border into political authority.

The formulation is architectural rather than a literal endorsement of political monarchy.

## Problem pressure

The thread needed a memorable constitutional image that could preserve a powerful ego without granting that ego authority over other persons.

The broader thread was trying to preserve exceptional intellectual capacity without either converting it into jurisdiction over others or demanding that the person erase the ego that carries the capacity.

## Core model

~~~text
INTERIOR: monarchy
  ego / myth / ambition / theory may be extravagant

BORDER: constitution
  crossing requires rules / consent / contestability

EXTERIOR: republic
  other persons retain sovereignty
~~~

## Claims and implications

It relocates ethical discipline from making the ego democratic to making social crossings constitutional.

This entry belongs to the architecture of **psychic sovereignty, epistemic submission, and social non-domination**.

## Examples

### Clean example

A person permits themselves enormous private ambition but treats collaboration, leadership, and institutional intervention as domains requiring consent and challenge.

### Borderline example

A case in which similar outward behavior exists but the underlying motive, exit option, or constitutional status differs.

## Non-examples

Using private self-conception as evidence of political entitlement.

## Boundaries and failure modes

A psychologically monarchical interior can still generate external behavior indirectly; the framework depends on robust borders rather than wishful separation.

## Operationalization / evidence

Track the concrete features named by the concept rather than inferring equivalence from appearances alone. For dependency claims, inspect credible exit and fallback options. For epistemic claims, inspect actual response to correction. For psychic-governance claims, distinguish internal desire from external imposition.
