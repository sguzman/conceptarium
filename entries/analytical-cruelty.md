# Analytical cruelty

## Definition

**Analytical cruelty** occurs when the language or machinery of truth-seeking is used to legitimize unnecessary suffering.

The analytical process may contain valid evidence while also adding punitive surplus.

~~~text
evidence
    +
interpretation
    +
humiliation / contempt / punishment
        ↓
analytical cruelty
~~~

## Broader than cruelty in the tribunal

[Cruelty in the tribunal](./cruelty-in-the-tribunal.md) is the adjudicative subtype.

Analytical cruelty also includes cruelty occurring during:

- questioning;
- diagnosis;
- interpretation;
- provenance tracing;
- exposure;
- explanation.

## Why the distinction matters

A person can be correct and cruel at the same time.

Discovering punitive motive does not automatically falsify the conclusion.

The task is to separate:

- what is true;
- what must be constrained;
- what suffering was gratuitously added.

## Common forms

- humiliating language that adds no evidentiary value;
- interpreting ambiguity in the most degrading way;
- treating pain as proof of honesty;
- assuming mercy contaminates truth;
- erasing standing after one harmful motive is found.

## Relation to evidence versus punishment

[Evidence versus punishment](./evidence-versus-punishment.md) is a direct corrective.

An uncomfortable motive can be evidence without itself constituting a sentence.
