# Prestige without sovereignty

## Definition

A social arrangement in which excellence and achievement may produce admiration or recognition without automatically producing generalized authority.

Prestige can remain socially meaningful while its jurisdictional consequences are deliberately limited.

## Problem pressure

The thread wanted to satisfy legitimate recognition hunger without letting recognition harden into entitlement or command.

The broader thread was trying to preserve exceptional intellectual capacity without either converting it into jurisdiction over others or demanding that the person erase the ego that carries the capacity.

## Core model

~~~text
achievement / excellence
      ↓
prestige / admiration
      ✕
no generalized sovereignty
~~~

## Claims and implications

Status need not be abolished to prevent domination; the key design problem is preventing symbolic rank from automatically converting into broad authority.

This entry belongs to the larger architecture of **psychic sovereignty, epistemic submission, and social non-domination**.

## Examples

### Clean example

A celebrated artist receives honor and attention but no special legal or organizational authority outside clearly relevant roles.

### Borderline example

A case in which similar surface behavior exists but the underlying status, optionality, or jurisdictional structure differs.

## Non-examples

A celebrity’s prestige being treated as qualification to command unrelated institutions.

## Boundaries and failure modes

Informal prestige can still produce de facto power even without formal office. The arrangement must account for influence as well as legal authority.

Do not treat Fallen/Village vocabulary as a biological caste or proof of moral superiority.

## Operationalization / evidence

Where empirical, examine actual options, observable status dynamics, reflexive behavior, institutional authority, and whether prestige or representation is converted into external constraint. Where normative, ask whether the rule preserves equal standing without falsifying real asymmetries.
