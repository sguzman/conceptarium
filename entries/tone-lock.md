# tone lock

## Definition

**Tone lock** is a temporary state in which media with one affective tone or atmosphere become disproportionately admissible while differently toned works become difficult to want.

Unlike genre lock, tone lock can cross genre boundaries.

A person may not need “science fiction” as such. They may need:

> brooding, nocturnal, high-contrast, melancholy media with a small ember of hope.

A detective story, horror game, cyberpunk RPG, or animated film could all satisfy the same tone lock.

## Problem pressure

The Genre lock entry explicitly noticed that the science-fiction appetite was narrower than genre. Two works could share the same genre while differing radically in suitability because one had the desired tone and the other did not.

That made tone a candidate independent gating dimension.

## Core model

~~~text
current affective need
      ↓
tone bundle becomes privileged
      ↓
matching works across genres gain access
      ↓
nonmatching tones become high-friction
~~~

## Claims and implications

Tone lock is a subtype of appetite narrowing, not necessarily a subtype of genre lock.

Genre and tone can align, but either can dominate. A strong tone lock may make cross-genre works more attractive than genre-matching works with the wrong mood.

## Examples

### Clean example

A player wants only brooding, rainy, melancholy media and happily moves between cyberpunk, supernatural mystery, and noir because all share the same emotional texture.

### Borderline example

A viewer generally loves tragic stories. That is stable taste unless the preference becomes temporarily restrictive.

### Interaction example

A science-fiction genre lock plus a brooding tone lock produces an unusually narrow admissible set.

## Non-examples

Simply preferring serious works to comedy over many years is not necessarily a tone lock.

A work being emotionally intense is not sufficient; the lock concerns the consumer’s temporary selection state.

## Boundaries and failure modes

Tone can be difficult to distinguish from aesthetic style, pacing, or theme. The concept should focus on **affective atmosphere** rather than every formal property.

If visual and sonic texture dominate while affect is secondary, **Aesthetic lock** may be the better label.

## Operationalization / evidence

Track cross-genre choices that converge on emotional texture. A clean tone-lock signature is rejection of same-genre works with the wrong mood combined with acceptance of cross-genre works with the right mood.
