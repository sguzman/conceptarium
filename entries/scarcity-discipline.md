# Scarcity discipline

## Definition

**Scarcity discipline** is behavioral restraint whose practical force comes substantially from external limits. The person consumes less because consumption is expensive, difficult, unavailable, time-consuming, socially prohibited, or materially impossible.

The term does not imply the person lacks internal discipline. It isolates the portion of restraint that scarcity is supplying on their behalf.

The compact originating definition was:

> Restraint produced or reinforced by external constraints such as cost, limited access, time, effort, or material shortage.

## Problem pressure

The concept was needed because identical behavior can be produced by radically different mechanisms. Two people may both eat modestly; one faces limited access while the other faces abundance and chooses restraint. Observing the outcome alone can hide the architecture that produced it.

## Core model / mechanism

```text
desire
  +
external constraint
        ↓
consumption ceiling
        ↓
apparent restraint
```

When the ceiling disappears, behavior becomes a test of whether an internal regulator exists.

## Claims and implications

Scarcity discipline can be socially useful and personally stabilizing even when it is not internally authored. The danger is misclassifying constraint-dependent behavior as a portable virtue.

## Examples

A household budget limiting restaurant meals; rural geography limiting junk-food access; slow travel limiting tourism frequency; a physical library limiting the number of books immediately available.

## Non-examples

A person with unrestricted access who deliberately stops; a person who simply dislikes the good; medically imposed inability to consume that is not experienced as a choice.

## Boundaries and failure modes

External and internal restraint often coexist. The concept is an analytic decomposition, not a binary personality type. Do not use it to insult people living under scarcity or to romanticize hardship.

## Operationalization / evidence

Compare behavior before and after changes in access, price, convenience, or time cost. If restraint collapses when the external ceiling disappears, scarcity had been doing substantial regulatory work.

## Relations

Scarcity discipline is the direct contrast class for **abundance discipline**. The larger post-scarcity framework asks what happens when scarcity discipline weakens and must be replaced or supplemented by internal or synthetic constraint.
