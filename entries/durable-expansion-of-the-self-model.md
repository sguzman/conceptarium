# Durable expansion of the self-model

## Definition

**Durable expansion of the self-model** is a persistent enlargement of the set of psychological states, motives, identities, desires, or capacities that a person can seriously consider as belonging to their own possible experience.

The persistence criterion matters:

~~~text
new possibility appears
      ↓
initiating state ends
      ↓
possibility remains thinkable
      ↓
future interpretation can still use it
      ↓
DURABLE SELF-MODEL EXPANSION
~~~

The concept is the strongest local test that an apparent opening changed more than the moment.

## Problem pressure

A dramatic experience can feel revelatory while it is happening.

But the later question is:

> **What remains after the state is gone?**

If nothing about the person's future hypothesis space changes, the event may have been intense, meaningful, or disinhibiting without producing durable expansion.

If the possibility remains available, the prior self-model cannot fully return to its older boundary.

## Conceivability is the minimum residue

Durable expansion does not require:

- adopting a permanent identity;
- endorsing the newly visible impulse;
- repeating the behavior;
- believing the first interpretation;
- preserving the same emotional intensity.

The minimum durable residue is weaker:

> “I can no longer honestly claim that this possibility is psychologically impossible for me.”

That is enough to enlarge future self-analysis.

## Expansion does not settle ontology

Persistence does not prove that the newly admitted material was:

- innate;
- latent from childhood;
- socially constructed;
- situationally produced;
- biologically caused;
- authentically “truer” than prior states.

The concept records a change in the **model's possibility set**, not a final theory of the self.

## Relation to admissibility

A possibility can persist at different levels:

### Conceivable

“I know this state is possible.”

### Admissible

“I accept that it can legitimately count as evidence about me.”

### Integrated

“I have reorganized my broader self-understanding around it.”

Durable expansion can begin at the first level and deepen later.

## Relation to opening

Opening is the transition.

Durable expansion is one possible persistent result.

~~~text
OPENING
boundary crossed

DURABLE EXPANSION
new boundary remains after the crossing event
~~~

This distinction allows failed, partial, or transient openings to be discussed without contradiction.

## Relation to disinhibition

A disinhibited state can produce behavior that disappears with the state.

That is not durable expansion.

But if the event permanently changes what the person considers possible, disinhibition has become evidence inside a larger opening.

## Relation to maintenance

Once expansion has occurred, the problem can shift from discovery to retention and integration.

Maintenance may help preserve access under later pressure, but the durable expansion should not be defined merely by continuous repetition of the initiating event.

## Examples

A person once unable to imagine themselves feeling a certain emotion can later recognize the possibility even in ordinary states.

An identity possibility first encountered during unusual circumstances remains a legitimate hypothesis years later, even if the person never settles on it as a permanent identity.

A previously excluded motive becomes part of the person's standard interpretive repertoire after one major contradiction of the old self-model.

## Non-examples

Durable expansion is not:

- a vivid memory alone;
- repeated behavior without self-model change;
- temporary disinhibition;
- permanent endorsement;
- irreversible identity commitment;
- proof that the first explanation was correct.

## Boundaries and failure modes

### Persistence equals truth

A durable belief can still be wrong.

### One-event overfitting

A single state may be given excessive weight.

### Identity foreclosure

Recognizing a possibility should not force premature permanent categorization.

### Forgetting context

Durable expansion should preserve provenance rather than erase the conditions under which the possibility first appeared.

## Operationalization

Evidence includes:

- persistence across later ordinary states;
- future spontaneous recognition of the possibility;
- stable change in self-description;
- reduced automatic rejection of related evidence;
- use of the new possibility in later reasoning;
- reinterpretation of earlier experience through the enlarged model.

## Relations

**Opening** can produce durable expansion.

**Disinhibition** contrasts with it because lowered restraint can disappear without residue.

**Expansion of the conscious mind** is the broader process of which durable self-model enlargement is a particularly important outcome.

**Maintenance** may help preserve access after the discovery phase.
