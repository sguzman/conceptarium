# Post-scarcity prestige ecology

## Definition

**Post-scarcity prestige ecology** is the umbrella framework for status competition after ordinary material access ceases to be the dominant scarcity. It asks not whether hierarchy disappears, but where prestige relocates when possession becomes cheap.

Candidate prestige objects include restraint, attention, authenticity, endurance, health maintenance, difficulty, slowness, privacy, selectivity, embodied competence, and credible refusal.

The compact originating definition was:

> The status system that emerges after ordinary material goods cease being meaningfully scarce, causing prestige to reorganize around scarce capacities such as restraint, attention, authenticity, endurance, or difficulty.

## Problem pressure

The motivating thought experiment began with limitless food and medicine. If everyone can consume richly, the old aristocratic signal of abundance collapses. Yet status competition need not vanish. Thinness, discipline, friction, or non-consumption may become premium precisely because indulgence is universal.

## Core model / mechanism

```text
material scarcity falls
        ↓
possession loses signaling power
        ↓
pleasure saturation
        ↓
prestige migration
        ↓
scarce capacities become status-bearing
        ↓
abstention / friction / authenticity / maintenance
        ↓
new abundance sorting
```

## Claims and implications

The framework predicts persistence of positional competition under abundance, but not any single inevitable hierarchy. It is a research program for asking what remains scarce once goods are not.

## Examples

A world of free food where body maintenance and selective eating become elite signals; ubiquitous AI where original attention and source provenance become premium; instant travel where difficult remote journeys become luxury experiences.

## Non-examples

A conventional luxury economy where prestige still mainly tracks costly possession; a truly statusless society; scarcity regimes where basic access remains the dominant social cleavage.

## Boundaries and failure modes

“Post-scarcity” is domain-specific and approximate. Material abundance can coexist with scarce land, attention, relationships, political power, beauty, health, or time. The framework should not smuggle those remaining scarcities out of view.

## Operationalization / evidence

Study domains where access has already radically expanded—calories, music, images, information, computation—and test whether prestige shifts toward curation, restraint, authenticity, effort, or exclusivity.
