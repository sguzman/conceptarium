# Treating people as components

## Definition

**Treating people as components** is the failure mode in which a person is represented and acted upon primarily as an element inside another actor's system rather than as an independent center of projects, interpretation, consent, and standing.

~~~text
person
  ↓
modeled role / variable / resource / dependency
  ↓
their own purposes become implementation details
  ↓
system optimization proceeds over them
~~~

The problem is not modeling people.

The problem is **collapsing the difference between modeling a person and acquiring jurisdiction over them**.

## Problem pressure

The concept arose from a problem internal to system-building.

System-building can provide safety, predictability, efficiency, coherence, causal legibility, reduced friction, and better coordination.

If those are goods, an ambitious builder may naturally ask:

> Why should optimization stop at other people?

Nothing in optimization as such supplies an answer.

A sufficiently powerful planner can redescribe another person's time, labor, preferences, relationships, routines, body, attention, emotional state, or community as system variables.

The missing constraint is that the other person is not merely substrate.

## The reduction

Treating someone as a component often involves several moves:

~~~text
represent the person
        ↓
assign them a function
        ↓
optimize the larger system
        ↓
treat deviation as inefficiency
        ↓
subordinate their authored purposes
        ↓
preserve the system over their standing
~~~

The reduction can be bureaucratic, algorithmic, familial, interpersonal, political, or benevolent.

## Why “component”

A component is valuable primarily by the function it performs inside a larger system.

It can normally be replaced, optimized, constrained, moved, redesigned, monitored, or sacrificed for system performance.

Those operations are ordinary when applied to actual components.

The ethical failure appears when the same ontology is transferred to persons without preserving their independent standing.

## Modelability is not ownership

A system may model another person extremely well.

It may predict preferences, reactions, needs, risks, incentives, and likely future actions.

None of that produces title.

~~~text
better model
      ≠
more legitimate jurisdiction
~~~

This is the same boundary expressed by **imperium without jurisdiction** and **models don't confer title**.

## Benevolent componentization

The failure need not be hostile.

A parent, manager, institution, caregiver, or partner may genuinely believe:

> “I know what arrangement would be better for everyone.”

Componentization occurs when the affected person's ability to contest, refuse, reinterpret, or author their own project becomes secondary to the planner's model.

Good intentions do not eliminate the structural asymmetry.

## Institutional forms

### Bureaucratic componentization

A person becomes a case, score, slot, throughput unit, or eligibility object.

### Algorithmic componentization

A person becomes a feature vector whose predicted behavior is optimized without meaningful contestability.

### Workplace componentization

A worker is treated as interchangeable capacity while local knowledge, goals, or limits are ignored.

### Familial componentization

A family member's life is organized around preserving the household system regardless of their authored priorities.

### Political componentization

Citizens are treated as population inputs to be moved, disciplined, incentivized, or sacrificed for a system objective.

## Dependency is not componentization

People inevitably depend on one another.

Mutual dependence can be deeply human and non-dominating.

The issue is whether one party's system becomes **authoritatively prior** to the other's standing.

A person can voluntarily accept a constrained role while retaining meaningful consent, exit, appeal, negotiation, and recognition as a co-author.

That differs from unilateral componentization.

## Coordination is not componentization

Large systems require roles.

A hospital, school, software team, household, or government cannot operate if every interaction is renegotiated from first principles.

The question is not whether a person performs a function.

It is whether the function **exhausts their standing inside the decision architecture**.

## Sovereign-system alternative

[Sovereign system builders](./sovereign-system-builders.md) supplies the internal systems answer.

~~~text
my system
      encounters
another person's system
      ↓
not substrate
not mere variable
not owned component
      ↓
federation / negotiation / boundary / contestability
~~~

The key symmetry is:

> If the integrity of my constructed system matters, I need a reason why theirs does not.

## Anti-domination relation

Treating people as components violates [Anti-domination](./anti-domination.md) when the componentized person lacks practical resistance, exit, appeal, or counter-power.

This is especially severe when:

- the optimizer can rewrite options unilaterally;
- the person cannot inspect the model;
- refusal is treated as malfunction;
- system goals are not contestable;
- the person's own projects are invisible to the architecture.

## Totalization relation

[Totalization](./totalization.md) creates pressure toward componentization when a framework tries to absorb external persons into one comprehensive optimizing architecture.

But the concepts remain distinct.

A private theory can totalize without dominating anyone.

A local institution can componentize people without possessing a grand totalizing worldview.

## Examples

A workplace scheduling system treats workers solely as capacity units and systematically ignores declared constraints because only aggregate throughput matters.

A family organizer decides every member's role according to one theory of household flourishing and treats disagreement as irrational disruption.

An automated welfare system optimizes fraud reduction while giving claimants no meaningful avenue to challenge a false model.

A partner uses highly accurate psychological knowledge to manipulate another person's choices rather than make the relationship more contestable.

## Non-examples

Treating people as components is not:

- assigning roles;
- division of labor;
- planning;
- statistical modeling;
- predicting behavior;
- asking someone to fulfill obligations;
- temporary emergency command under justified conditions;
- all hierarchy.

The failure requires a **reduction of independent standing**, not merely functional coordination.

## Boundaries and failure modes

### Metaphor inflation

People are not literally systems or components. The metaphor is useful only if it preserves the moral distinction it was created to express.

### Autonomy absolutism

Respect for standing does not mean every preference overrides shared rules or other people's rights.

### Contestability theater

A nominal appeal process does not solve componentization if it cannot actually alter outcomes.

### Benevolence laundering

Claiming superior knowledge of someone's welfare cannot substitute for justification of authority.

### Collective componentization

A community itself can be treated as a component by a larger institution, but protecting community integrity must not erase individual standing inside the community.

## Operationalization / evidence

Indicators include:

- people represented only through system-relevant attributes;
- goals imposed without channels for revision;
- disagreement interpreted as noise or defect;
- weak or absent exit and appeal;
- optimization metrics that omit affected persons' own objectives;
- inability to inspect or contest the model;
- repeated sacrifice of local projects for aggregate system performance.

A strong diagnostic question is:

> **Does this person have standing to alter the system that is optimizing over them?**

## Relations

It violates **anti-domination** when reduction becomes practically unanswerable power.

**Constitutionalized border crossing** constrains the movement from private model to external intervention.

**Totalization** is a common but non-necessary route into componentization.
