# Epistemic review

## Definition

**Epistemic review** re-evaluates an old proposition or rule against present evidence and present conditions.

~~~text
old conclusion
    ↓
recover provenance
    ↓
reconstruct original evidence
    ↓
compare with current evidence
    ↓
retain / narrow / revise / retire
~~~

## Review is not repeal

The point is not to distrust the past automatically.

An old rule may survive review unchanged.

The important shift is from **inherited authority** to **reviewed authority**.

## Review questions

1. What proposition is active?
2. When was it formed?
3. What problem did it solve?
4. What evidence supported it?
5. What evidence exists now?
6. Which conditions changed?
7. What would falsify or narrow it?
8. What cost does continued enforcement create?

## Relation to reopening the case

[Reopening the case](./reopening-the-case.md) is the judicial metaphor.
