# Abundance politics

## Definition

**Abundance politics** is politics after allocation ceases to be the only or dominant problem in a domain. It concerns the governance of abundance itself: how available pleasures should be, what defaults surround them, which frictions are legitimate, what limits may be imposed, who designs temptation environments, and how sovereignty is protected.

It turns questions of self-regulation into institutional questions without assuming the institution should control the person.

The compact originating definition was:

> Politics centered less on allocating scarce goods and more on deciding which forms of friction, temptation, access, limitation, and self-regulation should exist.

## Problem pressure

Traditional political economy is organized heavily around scarcity: who gets what, at what price, and by what right. In a pleasure-abundant world, the central conflict can move toward who structures the environment of access and which constraints survive.

## Core model / mechanism

```text
access problem weakens
        ↓
governance problem changes
        ↓
debate over temptation / defaults / friction / limits
        ↓
institutional design of abundance
```

## Claims and implications

Abundance politics may divide around paternalism, autonomy, public health, commercial optimization, children, addictive design, and rights to frictionless access or chosen disconnection. It is not inherently left or right.

## Examples

Rules for hyper-palatable food design; rights to disable recommendation algorithms; public defaults around gambling-like mechanics; voluntary digital limits; debates over whether institutions may impose cooling-off periods.

## Non-examples

Ordinary rationing during shortage; taxation whose only purpose is redistributing scarce goods; private self-control with no institutional dimension.

## Boundaries and failure modes

A politics of self-regulation can easily become technocratic domination. The anti-domination requirement is strong: constraints need transparency, contestability, proportionality, and meaningful exit wherever possible.

## Operationalization / evidence

Look for domains where access has become cheap while political conflict increasingly concerns design, defaults, intensity, addiction, or restraint rather than distribution alone.

## Relations

Abundance politics is part of **post-scarcity prestige ecology**, uses **constraint substitution**, shapes the **temptation environment**, and determines the legitimate scope of **synthetic friction**.
