# Treasury fungibility / taxpayer sovereignty

## Definition

**Treasury fungibility / taxpayer sovereignty** is the political-institutional version of physical pooling / legal individuation.

It asserts that government need not choose between:

~~~text
efficient pooled treasury
        OR
meaningful contributor rights
~~~

A treasury can move cash for liquidity and budget execution while a separate legal/accounting layer preserves contributor attribution and bounded rights.

## Problem pressure

The source wanted to preserve the genuine administrative advantages of general funds without allowing those advantages to silently justify contributor disappearance.

## Core model

~~~text
TREASURY LAYER
fungible cash / pooled liquidity / budget execution

        +

RIGHTS LAYER
attribution / provenance / bounded allocation / standing
~~~

## Claims and implications

This is a compatibility thesis. The engineering case for pooling does not automatically defeat the normative case for taxpayer rights.

Some rights may still impose real operational constraints; compatibility is not costlessness.

## Examples

### Clean example

Treasury cash is swept across accounts for liquidity management while a legally authoritative attribution ledger preserves each taxpayer’s share of every appropriation.

### Borderline example

A government preserves provenance but no allocation rights. Treasury fungibility coexists with a thin form of taxpayer sovereignty.

## Non-examples

Aggregate spending percentages without individualized claims do not satisfy the sovereignty side. Rigid earmarking that prevents any pooling is not required.

## Boundaries and failure modes

The phrase taxpayer sovereignty can exaggerate if rights are purely informational. The exact bundle must be stated. A layered ledger can also obscure accountability if allocation rules are opaque.

## Operationalization / evidence

Demonstrate independent treasury liquidity operations and a separate rights ledger whose outputs remain stable under those operations.

## Relations

This distinction refines **Physical pooling / legal individuation**, supports **Taxpayer sovereignty**, and opposes the inference embodied by **Claim extinction**.
