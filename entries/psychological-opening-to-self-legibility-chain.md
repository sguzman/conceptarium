# Psychological closure → opening → expanded admissible self-model → self-interrogation → recursive introspection → investigation of the investigator → procedural scrutiny of the internal tribunal → increasing self-legibility without complete conscious sovereignty

## Definition

This entry preserves the full **psychological opening to self-legibility chain** as one first-class architecture:

~~~text
PSYCHOLOGICAL CLOSURE
        ↓
OPENING
        ↓
EXPANDED ADMISSIBLE SELF-MODEL
        ↓
SELF-INTERROGATION
        ↓
RECURSIVE INTROSPECTION
        ↓
INVESTIGATION OF THE INVESTIGATOR
        ↓
PROCEDURAL SCRUTINY OF THE INTERNAL TRIBUNAL
        ↓
INCREASING SELF-LEGIBILITY
        ↓
WITHOUT COMPLETE CONSCIOUS SOVEREIGNTY
~~~

The value of the chain is causal and constitutional.

It specifies not merely a list of concepts but **how one stage creates pressure for the next**.

## Why preserve the entire chain

Breaking the model into entries creates a risk that the overall movement disappears.

The chain protects several load-bearing claims simultaneously:

1. self-knowledge can be blocked by closure;
2. opening changes what can be seriously considered;
3. newly visible material must still receive standing;
4. self-analysis can itself contain hidden authorities;
5. those authorities must become investigable;
6. verdicts must be subjected to procedural review;
7. better procedure can increase self-legibility;
8. increased legibility still does not yield total conscious rule.

The final constraint is as important as the opening.

## Stage 1 — Psychological closure

[Psychological closure](./psychological-closure.md) narrows:

- psychological territory;
- search space;
- admissibility.

Some possibilities never become live hypotheses.

## Stage 2 — Opening

[Opening](./opening.md) crosses one of those boundaries.

A previously excluded possibility becomes available for examination.

The opening need not settle whether the material was discovered or created.

## Stage 3 — Expanded admissible self-model

The person can now say:

> “This possibility deserves to be included in the space of things I may genuinely be capable of.”

Admissibility is not approval.

It is standing as evidence.

## Stage 4 — Self-interrogation

The expanded self-model creates more objects for examination.

The person questions:

- motives;
- contradictions;
- memories;
- defenses;
- inherited rules.

Self-interrogation remains queued elsewhere as part of the larger self-authorship family, but it already has predicate presence and multiple provenance streams.

## Stage 5 — Recursive introspection

[Recursive introspection](./recursive-introspection.md) turns the analytical apparatus onto itself.

The interpreter becomes an object.

## Stage 6 — Investigation of the investigator

[Investigating the investigator](./investigating-the-investigator.md) tests the motives and standards of the reflective authority.

No claim to neutrality becomes self-authenticating.

## Stage 7 — Procedural scrutiny of the tribunal

[Procedural self-analysis](./procedural-self-analysis.md) audits:

- burdens of proof;
- evidentiary symmetry;
- reversibility;
- role reversal;
- punitive surplus.

The [Internal tribunal](./internal-tribunal.md) remains necessary but contestable.

## Stage 8 — Increasing self-legibility

[Legibility of the self](./legibility-of-the-self.md) grows as:

- inner states become differentiable;
- tribunal procedures become explicit;
- self-models become revisable;
- patterns can be compared over time.

## Final constraint — no complete conscious sovereignty

The chain terminates in a denial:

> **Increasing self-legibility does not imply complete conscious sovereignty.**

[Self-opacity](./self-opacity.md), [Retreating frontier](./retreating-frontier.md), and state dependence preserve the limit.

The result is:

~~~text
MORE ACCESS
MORE MAP
MORE PROCEDURE
MORE SELF-KNOWLEDGE
        ≠
TOTAL MASTERY
~~~

## The constitutional pair

The chain carries two internal constitutional rules.

### Anti-immunity

> **No part of me gets to say: do not investigate me.**

### Anti-cruelty

Investigability does not grant torture authority.

The self becomes more available to inquiry while still requiring procedural limits on punishment and erasure.

## Discovery versus creation remains open

The chain describes changes in access and administration.

It does not settle whether newly available material was:

- latent;
- constructed;
- socially produced;
- imitated;
- reinforced;
- state-dependent;
- mixed.

That question is separately preserved in [Discovery versus creation of the self](./discovery-versus-creation-of-the-self.md).

## Non-examples

The chain is not:

- a universal developmental sequence for everyone;
- a clinical treatment protocol;
- proof that more introspection is always better;
- a claim that the unconscious can be fully conquered;
- a theory that every denied possibility is secretly true.

It is the architecture of one local research model.

## Boundaries and failure modes

### Linearization

Real psychological development can move backward, branch, or skip stages.

### Opening romanticism

Opening can destabilize without improving procedure.

### Investigation totalization

Universal examinability can become endless prosecution.

### Sovereignty rebound

Sophisticated maps can recreate the illusion of complete mastery.

### Historical universalization

The chain should not be projected onto all cultures or persons as one inevitable path.

## Operationalization

The architecture can be tested locally by asking:

- Did the search space actually expand?
- Did new possibilities remain admissible later?
- Did the investigator become an object of scrutiny?
- Did procedural standards become more explicit?
- Did self-legibility improve?
- Did confidence in total self-transparency decrease rather than inflate?

## Relations

The entry **contains** the central stages of the architecture.

It **contrasts with conscious sovereignty** because the final theoretical output is increasing access under permanent incompleteness.
