# Secularization of introspective machinery

## Definition

**Secularization of introspective machinery** is the migration or transformation of inward techniques from explicitly religious settings into secular intellectual and cultural forms.

The machinery can persist while its justification changes.

~~~text
religious self-examination
        ↓ transformed / translated
autobiography / essay / novel / philosophy
        ↓
moral psychology / clinical psychology / psychoanalysis
        ↓
secular theories and practices of self-knowledge
~~~

## Machinery rather than doctrine

The concept deliberately focuses on **operations**:

- confess;
- record;
- compare;
- classify;
- narrate;
- trace provenance;
- question motive;
- detect self-deception;
- revisit earlier judgments.

A secular practice may inherit some operations without inheriting the theology that once justified them.

## Not a simple one-way transfer

"Secularization" here does not mean that all modern psychology is disguised religion.

Possible histories include:

- direct inheritance;
- partial translation;
- independent reinvention;
- convergence;
- rejection followed by structural similarity.

The concept therefore remains provisional until specific genealogies are evidenced.

## Relation to self-legibility

Techniques become historically important when they increase the ability to preserve, compare, narrate, and reason about subjective material.

That is a cultural route toward [Legibility of the self](./legibility-of-the-self.md).

## Boundaries

Do not infer genealogy from resemblance alone.

Structural similarity is evidence for comparison, not proof of descent.
