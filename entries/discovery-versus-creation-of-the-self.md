# Discovery versus creation of the self

## Definition

**Discovery versus creation of the self** is the unresolved problem of what kind of event psychological opening actually reveals.

When a new identity, desire, affective register, role, or self-description becomes accessible, several explanations remain possible.

~~~text
NEWLY ACCESSIBLE MATERIAL
        ↓
        ├── previously latent and discovered
        ├── weakly present but newly named
        ├── socially scaffolded into existence
        ├── experimentally constructed
        ├── state-dependent
        ├── reinforced into stability
        └── mixture of several processes
~~~

The concept exists to prevent one phenomenological fact—

> “This possibility became real enough for me to experience.”

—from settling the causal ontology automatically.

## Problem pressure

Opening can create powerful certainty.

A person may feel:

> “I discovered what was always there.”

or:

> “This was created by circumstances, therefore it is fake.”

Both moves can exceed the evidence.

The first can erase construction and context.

The second can erase genuine present experience merely because its provenance is contingent.

## Discovery hypothesis

The strongest discovery model says:

~~~text
trait / desire / identity already existed
        ↓
closure prevented access
        ↓
opening removed barrier
        ↓
latent material became conscious
~~~

This model may fit some cases.

It should not be universalized automatically.

## Creation hypothesis

A construction model says:

~~~text
new context / language / practice / imitation / reinforcement
        ↓
new pattern forms
        ↓
pattern becomes experientially real
~~~

“Constructed” does not mean “fake.”

Many durable human capacities are constructed.

## Mixed model

The most plausible cases may combine:

- predisposition;
- social example;
- conceptual vocabulary;
- experimentation;
- reinforcement;
- state dependence;
- later integration.

The framework therefore resists binary metaphysics.

## Relation to opening

[Opening](./opening.md) tells us that the search space changed.

It does not tell us why the newly accessible possibility exists.

That is the separation:

~~~text
phenomenological result
        ≠
settled developmental cause
~~~

## Relation to durable expansion

A possibility can become durably incorporated regardless of whether its origin was discovery, construction, or mixture.

Persistence is evidence of present model significance, not proof of timeless latent essence.

## Relation to provenance and legitimacy

The queued **Provenance–legitimacy distinction** was captured to express a nearby rule:

> where a behavior or impulse came from does not by itself determine whether it legitimately belongs in present self-understanding.

That record is currently under duplicate-consolidation pressure with **Provenance ≠ legitimacy** and therefore remains unmaterialized.

## Self-opacity

[Self-opacity](./self-opacity.md) means the causal history may remain unresolved even after extensive analysis.

Ontological suspension can therefore be a legitimate endpoint.

## Examples

A behavior first learned by imitation becomes a durable and personally meaningful expressive mode.

A previously unthinkable identity becomes available only after exposure to a new social category.

A recurring impulse appears under multiple conditions long before the person has language for it, supporting some discovery interpretation without proving a simple innate essence.

## Non-examples

The framework is not:

- a claim that all identity is constructed;
- a claim that all identity is innate;
- permission to dismiss experience as imitation;
- proof that durability equals innateness;
- proof that social causation makes something unreal.

## Boundaries and failure modes

### Authenticity-by-origin

Treating innate or early origin as the only route to legitimacy.

### Construction-as-fakery

Assuming learned behavior cannot become genuinely one's own.

### Discovery romanticism

Retrospectively rewriting every earlier event as evidence of one latent essence.

### Causal nihilism

Keeping ontology open does not forbid weighing evidence.

## Operationalization

A serious inquiry can compare:

- earliest known occurrences;
- behavior across states;
- behavior before relevant social exposure;
- persistence after initiating conditions end;
- reinforcement history;
- alternative explanations;
- retrospective memory reliability.

## Relations

**Opening** motivates the question.

**Durable expansion of the self-model** supplies evidence of persistence.

The unresolved **Provenance–legitimacy distinction** is a neighboring rule.

**Self-opacity** limits how completely causal origin can be recovered.
