# Abundance sorting

## Definition

**Abundance sorting** is differentiation that emerges after access is broadly equalized but responses to access remain heterogeneous. People sort by consumption patterns, self-regulation, attention, maintenance, taste, risk, or use of abundance rather than by possession alone.

It shifts the inequality question from **who gets the good?** to **what happens to different people once everyone gets it?**

The compact originating definition was:

> Social differentiation produced by how individuals respond to universal access rather than by unequal access itself.

## Problem pressure

Post-scarcity often imagines equal access as the end of sorting. The counter-puzzle is that universal access can expose or create differences in behavior, capacities, bodies, and status. Equal inputs need not yield equal trajectories.

## Core model / mechanism

```text
access equalized
      ↓
heterogeneous response
      ↓
cumulative behavioral divergence
      ↓
different outcomes
      ↓
new social interpretation / status
```

## Claims and implications

Abundance sorting can be benign, tragic, self-authored, structurally shaped, or exploitative. The term does not imply outcomes are deserved. It merely relocates one source of differentiation from access to response.

## Examples

Universal entertainment access producing differences in attention discipline; abundant food producing different long-term consumption patterns; universal educational tools producing divergent use patterns; equalized medical access combined with divergent maintenance behavior.

## Non-examples

Classic class sorting caused by unequal wealth; unequal health caused primarily by different healthcare access; arbitrary discrimination unrelated to how abundance is used.

## Boundaries and failure modes

The biggest danger is moral laundering. “Response” is still shaped by childhood, stress, disability, environment, culture, algorithms, and institutions. Equal formal access does not make every downstream difference purely individual.

## Operationalization / evidence

Hold access approximately constant and measure variance in usage, self-regulation, outcomes, and status. Decompose environmental and behavioral contributors rather than treating residual variance as free choice.

## Relations

It is a major output of **pleasure ecology**, a specialization within **post-scarcity prestige ecology**, and the broader category under which **longevity sorting** sits.
