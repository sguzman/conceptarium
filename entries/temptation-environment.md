# Temptation environment

## Definition

A **temptation environment** is the structured field of cues, access costs, defaults, intensity, timing, recommendation systems, social norms, and physical placement that determines how often and how strongly desirable options press for action.

It shifts analysis away from treating temptation as an isolated internal event.

The compact originating definition was:

> The surrounding system that determines how available, immediate, intense, and difficult-to-resist pleasurable options are.

## Problem pressure

If abundance discipline is tested by access, then access itself needs structure. Unlimited goods can be passive or aggressively optimized to seize attention. The environment determines whether self-regulation faces occasional invitations or continuous high-intensity assault.

## Core model / mechanism

```text
availability
+ immediacy
+ cue density
+ reward intensity
+ personalization
+ switching cost
        ↓
temptation load on the agent
```

## Claims and implications

Individual discipline and environment interact. Stronger environments can make the same internal capacity look weaker; supportive environments can make regulation easier without eliminating choice.

## Examples

A home filled with visible snack food; an autoplaying personalized feed; a casino floor; a quiet device-free room; a platform where every reward is one click away.

## Non-examples

A desire arising with no environmental cue at all; a good that is technically available but practically invisible and effortful; mere existence of pleasure somewhere in society.

## Boundaries and failure modes

Do not use environmental explanation to erase agency, or agency language to erase environment. The concept exists precisely to model their interaction.

## Operationalization / evidence

Quantify cue frequency, delay to reward, number of steps required, personalization, sensory intensity, availability hours, and default exposure; compare behavior under controlled environmental changes.

## Relations

The temptation environment is shaped by **pleasure ecology** and **abundance politics**, constrains the practical difficulty of **abundance discipline**, and helps produce patterns of **abundance sorting**.
